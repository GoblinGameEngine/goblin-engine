                                CURRY CREEK BRIDGE
                         (GDOT Bridge #157/00015/012.80N)                    (o
                                    HAER GA-67

Location:         Spanning Curry Creek on State Route 15, Jefferson, Jackson
                  County, Georgia

Date of
  Construction;   1926

Present Owner:    State of Georgia

Present Use:      Vehicular Bridge

Significance:     Curry Creek Bridge represents the solid concrete arch
                  bridge type. One of forty-six concrete arch bridges
                  recorded in the state of Georgia and one of thirty-seven
                  identified as a solid concrete arch, it is the only such
                  bridge recorded in Jackson County.

                  This bridge does not possess a bridge plate. No
                  infornation has been found concerning the history of the
                  bri.dge with the exception of the construction date.
                  According to Ms. Ann A. Jarrett of the Jackson County
                  Historical Society, the bridge was built in 1926 to replace
                  a covered bridge at the same location.

Sources:          Ann A. Jarrett, Jackson County Historical Society

                  Elrod, Frary. Historical Notes on Jackson County,
                  Georgia. Jefferson (Georgia?): no publisher given, 1967.

Historian:        Susan Shepherd Prosser, October, 1986.
