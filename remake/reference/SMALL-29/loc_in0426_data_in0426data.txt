     Description:   The existing Main Street bridge spans the Little Vermilion River in
                    Section 26, Township 17 North, Range 9 West, as shown on the
                    Newport, Indiana 7.5 minute U.S.G.S. quadrangle map. The
                    structure provides the only access across the Little Vermilion River
                    on the north side of Newport. The surrounding land is primarily
                    undeveloped with residential and agricultural uses. North of the
                    bridge, agricultural and woods dominate. To the south is the
                    town of Newport with various residential units.

                    The Main Street bridge is a reinforced filled concrete arch
                    structure built in 1913 by Van Fossan and Noble of Parke County,
                    Indiana. The two span structure is 208 feet in length with an out-
                    to-out width of 20.8 feet that provides one 18.7 foot travel lane.
                    The spandrel walls are solid and the parapet walls are paneled.
                    Concrete standards grace the walls' endposts.

     History:       On April 7', 1913 the Vermillion County Board of Commissioners
                    awarded the contract to construct the bridge on Main Street over
•"                  the Little Vermilion River to Van Fossan and Noble for the sum of
                    $8,300.00. The new bridge on Main Street was intended to
                    replace an existing bridge on Popular Street which was to the
                    west three (3) city blocks. On August 13, 1913, the Vermillion
                    County Board of Commissioners appointed Mr. James Chipps as
                    superintendent of the project, representing the county. The
                    contractor began construction in the summer of 1913. Work on
                    the structure was suspended in December of 1913 due to the
                    winter weather. The bridge was essentially completed, except for
                    work on the banisters. The bridge was completed in the spring of
                    1914. Upon completion of the Main Street bridge, the covered
                    bridge on Popular Street {J.J. Daniels 1868) was taken down.

                    The Main Street bridge is currently the only structure to provide
                    access north over the Little Vermilion River. The structure is in
                    fair condition with signs of deterioration. The center pier within
                    the channel has some spalling damage on the west end. The
                    wingwall on the southeast corner of the structure has cracked and
                    separated from the bridge. The current sufficiency rating of the
                    structure is 31.8; a rating below 50 warrants replacement. The
                    structure contains only one vehicular lane and the posted weight
                    limit is 5 tons. The deteriorating condition of the bridge and
C                   substandard design warrants the replacement of the structure.

    In the summer of 1980, a series of repairs was made to maintain
    the structure.      On June 2, 1980, the Vermillion County
    Commissioners signed a contract with White Excavating Inc. of
    Clinton, Indiana, for $103,979.05 to perform the repairs on the
    structure. Design plans for the repairs were developed by Beam,
    Longest & Neff, Inc., Consulting Engineers of Indianapolis,
    Indiana. Repairs to the structure included removal of the existing
    roadway surface, remove and replace portions of the spandrel
    wall, repair vertical cracks, repoint masonry at various locations
    and install new guardrail on both ends of the structure. Repairs
    to the structure were completed in the fall of 1980.
