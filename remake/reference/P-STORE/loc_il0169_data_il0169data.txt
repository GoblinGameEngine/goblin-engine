               his death in 1860, when the property was
               inherited by his wife, Elizabeth Daman. She
#
               retained possession of the property until
               November 2, 1867, when it passed to Fayette
               H. Bennett.
                    On February 10, 1868, the property was
               conveyed to Lydia Thompson, whose husband
               installed the shop front, and used the build-
               ing as a store and residence.
                     They continued to use the building until
               September 26, 1894, when William H. Curtiss
               acquired the property, His widow is the pres-
               ent owner and with her son, Mr. R.E- Curtis,
               uses the building as a residence, to this
               date.
