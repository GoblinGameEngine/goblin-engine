    SIGNIFICANCE (ARCHITECTURAL AND HISTORICAL, INCLUDE ORIGINAL USE OF STRUCTURE)
    The builder of this fine little Greek house was one of the owners of the first cold steel
    plow factory, along with John Deere, pioneer developer of agricultural implements. The
    house is unusual for its interesting grilled frieze windows and delicately detailed doorway
