    SIGNIFICANCE (ARCHITECTURAL AND HISTORICAL,                     INCLUDE ORIGINAL USt OF SiKUUURt)
       This is a good example of the bungalow house type that swept the county in the 1910s
       and the 1920s. Although less aesthetically important than the Prarie style that was
       developing during the same period, the bungalow became far more, popular and affordable
       to working-class Americans.                             ,
    STYLE (IF APPROPRIATE)
       Bungalow
    MATERIAL OF CONSTRUCTION (INCLUDE STRUCTURAL SYSTEMS)
       The house is laid on a brick foundation with wood framing and clapboard above.

      APE AND DIMENSIONS 0.F STRUCTURE (SK * :HED FLOOR PLANS ON SEPARATE PAGES ARE ACCEPTABLE)
       Rectangular in plan, the one-and-a-half srtory house contains a large, simple pitched
       gable roof.
    EXTERIOR FEATURES OF NOTE
       Simple in decoration, the clapboard is very narrow and articulated with corner boards.
       The gable roof with the gable end towards the street and the gable over the
       projecting enclosed entrance porch contain large overhangs; the wide eaves contain
       straight brackets.
