## Sources

- **Wikipedia infoboxes** (en.wikipedia.org, "Demographics" sections citing the 2020 U.S. Census / 2016–2020 ACS) were used for all population trend figures (2010 vs. 2020 Census) and for median age / % under 18 / % 65+, since these reflect the official decennial count rather than a small-sample estimate.
- **City-Data.com** city profile pages (and their linked `/housing/houses-*` subpages) were used for median household income, poverty rate, homeownership rate, average household size, employment-sector breakdowns, and educational attainment — all 2024 ACS 5-year estimates. City-Data was the more useful source for exactly the categories Wikipedia's infoboxes lack (income beyond the 2000 Census, current poverty, employment sectors, education), consistent with expectations for villages this small.
- **data.census.gov** was not queried directly in this pass (City-Data's ACS-derived figures and Wikipedia's Census-derived figures covered the requested fields efficiently); if higher-precision or margin-of-error data is later needed, data.census.gov ACS 5-year tables (S1901, S1701, DP02/DP04) are the authoritative next step.
- **Data gaps**: % Bachelor's+ could not be retrieved for Buda, Toulon, Roanoke, New Hampton, or Hoopeston (City-Data's educational-attainment chart is rendered as an image/interactive widget that the fetch tool could not read numeric values from — the field exists on the live page but wasn't extractable here). Average household size is "n/a" for Roanoke and Hoopeston for the same reason. Several villages (Wyanet, Plainview) show a notable gap between the 2020-Census-period ACS figure and the 2024 City-Data estimate for income/poverty/household size — likely margin-of-error noise typical of ACS estimates in populations under ~1,500 — both figures are noted where this occurred. Plainview's age breakdown is from the 2010 Census (Wikipedia's article had not been updated with 2020 figures at the time of this research).
- **South Haven and Saugatuck (Part 3 additions)**: full per-town research files with physical-layout detail live at `../community_profiles/south_haven_michigan.md` and `../community_profiles/saugatuck_michigan.md` (also copied into this tier's `population_tiers/villages_500_5000/` folder). Both are Lake Michigan resort towns — note their demographic profile (very high median age, very high homeownership, small household size) is structurally different from every farm-belt village above despite similar population size; see `_demographic_archetypes.md` for the Lake-Resort/Tourism archetype signature this produces.
