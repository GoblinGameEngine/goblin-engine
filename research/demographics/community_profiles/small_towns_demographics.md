## Sources

- **Wikipedia infoboxes/demographics sections** (cite U.S. Census decennial and ACS data) — primary source for population trend, median age, age distribution, and older (2000/2010-era) income and poverty figures for all 12 towns.
- **City-Data.com city profile pages** (2024 estimates) — primary source for current median household income, poverty rate, top employment industries, and average household size where Wikipedia lacked current figures.
- **data.census.gov / Census QuickFacts** — attempted directly but blocked automated fetch access (HTTP 403) for all towns; not used as a direct source this pass.
- **bestplaces.net** — attempted for homeownership rate but pages redirected and could not be fetched.

**Data gaps flagged:**
- **Homeownership rate**: could not be confirmed for 11 of 12 towns (only Bryan, OH at 60.5%; Carroll, IA at 58.1% was found on a later pass — see below). City-Data pages render this as an interactive/dynamic chart not present in static fetches, and current-era (2020 census template) Wikipedia demographics sections no longer state it in prose the way older articles did.
- **% Bachelor's degree or higher**: only found for McCook, NE (25.5%) and Carroll, IA (27.1%); n/a for the other 10 towns for the same dynamic-field reason.
- **Average household size**: not found for Kendallville IN, Napoleon OH, and Litchfield MN.
- **Napoleon employment sectors** lack percentage breakdowns (only named major employers: Campbell's Soup plant, Tenneco Elastomer).
- Charles City, McCook, and Abingdon poverty rates were refreshed on a follow-up pass using City-Data 2024 estimates (now listed alongside the older figure for context); Fairfield's average household size was corrected from an erroneous 2.4 (Iowa's state average, mistakenly attributed to the town) to the town's own 2010-census figure of 2.09.
- Carroll, IA had the most complete City-Data profile of the 12: homeownership 58.1%, bachelor's+ 27.1%, avg. household size 2.2 all confirmed directly.
