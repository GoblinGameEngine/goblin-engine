3. BRIDGES  [ALL NEW]
================================================================================
Split into BIG (main river channel, ~45 m wide, few per station) and SMALL (tributary creeks
4-10 m wide / farm ditches 2-4 m wide, many per station per generator_rules.md §10's new
crossing-density rule — small-street crossings should substantially outnumber main-channel
ones). Every road class in generator_rules.md §2 that could plausibly meet a tributary or ditch
gets its own small-crossing variant below, rather than one generic "small bridge."

--- Big bridges (main river channel) ---
[NEW] Downtown river-crossing bridge (Main Street over the ~45 m river channel — needs to
      read as the town's primary bridge, per Charles City-style "Main Street crosses the
      river" pattern found in research)
[NEW] Secondary/residential street river-crossing bridge (narrower than the Main St bridge)
[NEW] Farm-road river-crossing bridge (rural, narrower, possibly single-lane)
[NEW] Rail crossing / trestle if a rail corridor is later added (flagged as optional — the
      rail-corridor downtown archetype and grain-elevator placement both imply a rail line)

--- Small bridges/crossings (tributary creeks, 4-10 m wide) ---
[NEW] Residential local-street tributary-creek bridge (small timber or single-span concrete
      deck, ~4-10 m span — this is the single most-repeated bridge type per generator_rules.md
      §10's new "≥2 local streets per named tributary" rule, so it needs enough visual variants
      to avoid looking stamped when the player crosses several in one walk)
[NEW] Downtown side-street/alley tributary-creek bridge (narrower urban variant, low parapet,
      matches the downtown streetscape rather than the rural residential one)
[NEW] Farm access-lane tributary-creek bridge (rural single-lane variant, distinct from the
      farm-road *river* bridge above — this one is ditch/creek scale, not channel scale)
[NEW] Footbridge (park/riverwalk pedestrian crossing over a tributary creek, main river
      narrow point, or a minor unnamed drainage spur)
[NEW] Minor unnamed drainage-spur crossing (smallest scale — a single-plank or narrow-span
      footbridge/culvert hybrid for the new 30-100 m dead-end spurs in generator_rules.md §10,
      which exist purely to add crossing-density scenery variety)

--- Culverts (no visible span — road passes over, not a bridge structure) ---
[NEW] Small culvert/creek crossing (road passes over a 4-10 m tributary creek with a buried
      pipe/box culvert instead of a full bridge — an alternate, cheaper-reading option to the
      small bridges above for lower-traffic crossings)
[NEW] Road-over-drainage-ditch culvert (simple culvert pipe under a farm road or residential
      street, smaller ditch scale, 2-4 m — the most numerous single crossing type given the
      120-200 m farm ditch grid spacing)

NOTES
================================================================================
- This list is derived entirely from the research phase; nothing here has been modeled yet
  except the items explicitly marked [EXISTING].
- The [EXISTING] marks came from a repo scan of godot_project/assets/ (editor_assets,
  downtown_assets, farm_assets manifests + all .glb files present) on 2026-09-22 — re-verify
  before starting Phase 2 in case assets changed since. Section 9 (added after a second
  research pass) was not re-checked against the asset scan since it's a wholly new category
  (individual yard props, not buildings) with no plausible existing overlap.
- Rail depot and founder's-mansion are gaps discovered while writing this checklist — they
  came up in the village-tier research (research/population_tiers/villages_500_5000/
  _tier_summary.md) but weren't included in either building_catalog pass. Worth a small
  follow-up research/template pass before Phase 2, or can be treated as one-off unique builds
  since both are explicitly "at most one per settlement" per the research.
- Marina/harbor infrastructure (docks, harbormaster building, fuel dock, chandlery-type retail)
  and a hand-cranked pedestrian chain ferry are two more building/prop gaps surfaced by the
  Great Lakes demographic-expansion research (research/demographics/_great_lakes_expansion_notes.md,
  items 3-4) — not in either building catalog, worth a follow-up pass alongside rail
  depot/founder's-mansion above if the station ever models a resort-village-style zone variant.
- Priority suggestion for Phase 2 (not a research finding, just a practical ordering note):
  the river/lake/pond water-feature system and the bridge set are the highest-leverage new
  work, since almost everything else (road sections, drainage, town-siting rules, and now the
  elevation/bluff system in generator_rules.md §16) depends on the river's geometry existing
  first.
- Section 9 (yard/lot props), section 10 (interior props), the expanded tree/fence lists in
  sections 6-7, and the small-bridge/crossing expansion in section 3 were all added in a
  second checklist pass after the user pointed out the original count omitted interiors
  entirely and under-specified small-street crossings and vegetation/fence variety. The
  overall new-model count is now **411** (146 exterior/infrastructure items in this file, up
  from the original 112, plus the 265-item interior props list) — see
  research/interior_props_master_list.md for the itemized interior breakdown, since it isn't
  reproduced line-by-line in this file.
- generator_rules.md §10 now has an explicit street-network generation rule requiring every
  named tributary creek to be crossed by at least 2 local/side streets, plus 1-2 minor
  unnamed drainage spurs per Residential/Farm zone segment purely for crossing-density variety
  — this is what drives section 3's expanded small-bridge/culvert list.
