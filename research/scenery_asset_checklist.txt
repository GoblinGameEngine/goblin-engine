NOTES
================================================================================
- This list is derived entirely from the research phase; nothing here has been modeled yet
  except the items explicitly marked [EXISTING].
- The [EXISTING] marks came from a repo scan of godot_project/assets/ (editor_assets,
  downtown_assets, farm_assets manifests + all .glb files present) on 2026-09-22 — re-verify
  before starting Phase 2 in case assets changed since.
- Rail depot and founder's-mansion are gaps discovered while writing this checklist — they
  came up in the village-tier research (research/population_tiers/villages_500_5000/
  _tier_summary.md) but weren't included in either building_catalog pass. Worth a small
  follow-up research/template pass before Phase 2, or can be treated as one-off unique builds
  since both are explicitly "at most one per settlement" per the research.
- Priority suggestion for Phase 2 (not a research finding, just a practical ordering note):
  the river/lake/pond water-feature system and the bridge set are the highest-leverage new
  work, since almost everything else (road sections, drainage, town-siting rules) depends on
  the river's geometry existing first.
