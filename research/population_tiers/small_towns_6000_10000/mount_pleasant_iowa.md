# Mount Pleasant, Iowa

Population: 9,274 (2020 census, up from 8,668 in 2010) — county seat of Henry County, southeast Iowa.

## Founding / water relationship
Founded 1835 by pioneer Presley Saunders; first settlers arrived 1833. Incorporated 1842, re-incorporated 1851. Like Fairfield, IA, this is a **ridge/upland county-center founding** rather than a river-mill site — the name itself ("Mount Pleasant," a scenic-high-ground name) signals the town was sited for topographic/agricultural appeal rather than water power, reinforcing that inland Iowa county seats commonly prioritized elevated, well-drained, centrally-located ground over water access.
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Pleasant,_Iowa)

## Downtown / Main Street layout
- **Mount Pleasant Downtown Historic District**, National Register listed — standard small Iowa county-seat commercial core.
- "Main Street Mount Pleasant" downtown organization actively promotes upper-story residential conversion above ground-floor retail — an increasingly common small-town revitalization pattern (converting long-vacant upper floors of 2-story Main St buildings into apartments), useful if the generator wants a "revitalizing downtown" variant with mixed ground-retail/upper-residential building interiors.
Source: [Mount Pleasant Downtown Historic District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Pleasant_Downtown_Historic_District), [Main Street Mount Pleasant](https://www.facebook.com/mainstreetmp/)
