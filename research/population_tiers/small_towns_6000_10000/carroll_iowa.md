# Carroll, Iowa

Population: 10,321 (2020 census); ~10,125 (2024 est.) — county seat of Carroll County, west-central Iowa. (Slightly above the tier's nominal 10,000 ceiling but included as a borderline/representative case — Iowa small-town scale is highly consistent across this size band.)

## Founding / water relationship
Laid out 1867 directly by the **Chicago and Northwestern Railroad** as "Carroll City," a planned railroad town — a purer version of the same rail-company-town founding pattern as McCook, NE. Became county seat in 1869, replacing the earlier town of Carrollton (a contested seat-relocation, common in Iowa/Nebraska county histories where the arrival of the railroad shifted which town became the county seat away from an earlier, non-rail-served settlement). A historic Chicago & Northwestern passenger depot (built 1896, replacing an earlier structure) still stands along the mainline.
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carroll,_Iowa), [Carroll Chamber history](https://www.carrolliowa.com/history-of-carroll)

## Downtown / Main Street layout
Downtown organized relative to the rail corridor and depot, consistent with the railroad-town archetype: commercial spine roughly parallel to the tracks, courthouse and civic buildings a short distance away on the platted grid.
Source: [Carroll Chamber history](https://www.carrolliowa.com/history-of-carroll)
