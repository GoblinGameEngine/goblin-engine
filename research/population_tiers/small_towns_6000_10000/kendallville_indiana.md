# Kendallville, Indiana

Population: ~10,222 (2022 est.; 2020 census figure lower, town has been growing) — Noble County, northeast Indiana.

## Founding / water relationship
Main Street began as a **buffalo/Native American trail**, later a plank road (built 1848) — an origin story distinct from both the river-mill and railroad-company patterns: the commercial spine follows a pre-existing overland travel corridor rather than a water crossing or a rail company's plat. First Main Street store opened 1840 at the NE corner of South Main and Gold Streets. William Mitchell ("father of Kendallville") platted 20 lots on the west side of the Main St business district in 1849 and gave them away free to anyone who would build and improve — an early "seed the downtown with free lots" growth tactic worth noting as a plausible founding-era mechanic (not a physical layout fact, but useful color).
Source: [Kendallville history](https://www.kendallvillein.gov/history), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendallville,_Indiana)

## Downtown / Main Street layout
- **Kendallville Downtown Historic District**, listed on the National Register Dec. 2003 (Indiana Register Oct. 2003 first) — standard small-town Main Street commercial historic district designation.
- Downtown organized along Main Street with cross streets including Gold Street near the historic core.
Source: [Kendallville Downtown Historic District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendallville_Downtown_Historic_District), [Kendallville Main Street org](https://www.kendallvillemainstreet.com/history)
