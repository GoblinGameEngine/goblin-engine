# Litchfield, Minnesota

Population: 6,624 (2020 census) — county seat of Meeker County, central Minnesota. (Near the tier's lower boundary — useful as the "small end" reference point.)

## Founding / water relationship
Platted by the **St. Paul and Pacific Railroad** in summer 1869; voted county seat that same fall — another clean railroad-company-town founding (same pattern as McCook, NE and Carroll, IA), named for a railroad contractor/investor/stockholder rather than a geographic feature. **Lake Ripley** sits on the south side of town; only about one-third of the lake's shoreline is residentially developed, with a public walking/biking path circling it — indicating the town has deliberately kept a large share of lakefront as public/undeveloped rather than fully privatized, a useful "partial-public-shoreline" zoning variant for the generator.
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litchfield,_Minnesota), [Litchfield Comprehensive Plan](https://www.ci.litchfield.mn.us/DocumentCenter/View/337/Chapter-1---Community-Profile-and-Introduction)

## Downtown / Main Street layout
Downtown properties appreciated quickly after founding; a building boom 1880-1900 produced large Italianate commercial structures, now the **Litchfield Commercial Historic District** (40 contributing properties) — a bigger/richer-than-average downtown building stock for the town's small size, similar to Abingdon VA's case of "historic wealth produced an oversized downtown relative to current population." Litchfield has also joined the Minnesota Main Street Program (active downtown-revitalization design assistance), same movement seen in Fairfield IA and Mount Pleasant IA.
Source: [WJON feature](https://wjon.com/historic-downtown-charm-the-story-of-litchfield/)
