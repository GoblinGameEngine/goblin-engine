# Plainview, Nebraska (Pierce County)

Population: 1,282 (2020 census); first official census in 1890 recorded 375. Land area 1.08 sq mi, 0 sq mi water. Computed density ≈ 1,187/sq mi. Coordinates 42°21′12″N 97°47′14″W, elevation 1,686 ft.

## Core layout
Railroad-platted town: settled 1871 (William B. Chilvers), post office/township established 1872 as "Roseville," renamed "Plainview" in 1874, formally platted in 1880 when the railroad extended to the site, incorporated April 1886. First census after incorporation (1890) recorded 375 residents — i.e., the town roughly **tripled from initial platted-era population (~375) to current (~1,282) over 130+ years**, unlike several Iowa/Illinois peers in this sample that peaked earlier and declined. Useful as a "still slowly growing" contrast case.

Notably, Plainview holds **three separate NRHP-listed properties** for a town this size: a historic railroad depot (now a museum), a Carnegie library (still operating), and a memorial band shell. Three distinct civic/cultural landmarks in a village of ~1,300 is a higher civic-landmark density than most peers in this sample — useful as a template for a "civic pride" village variant (old depot converted to museum, working Carnegie library, and a bandshell/park anchor near downtown) versus the more common bare-bones church/post-office/elevator core.
