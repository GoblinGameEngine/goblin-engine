# Toulon, Illinois (Stark County seat)

Population: 1,193 (2020 census); peaked at 1,400 in 2000. Land area 1.02 sq mi, 0 sq mi water. Density 1,174/sq mi (official). Coordinates 41.09556°N, 89.87333°W, elevation 735 ft.

## Core layout
Toulon is the **county seat of Stark County** — Illinois's smallest county by population — and its downtown is organized around the **Stark County Courthouse** (Greek Revival, built 1856-57) at 130 W. Main St. This is the courthouse-square town-form pattern, but at unusually small scale: the entire city is only 1.02 sq mi, so the courthouse square core dominates a proportionally larger share of the town than in bigger county seats. Toulon is also the northwestern terminus of the Rock Island Trail State Park (a rail-trail), confirming a former railroad presence now converted to a recreational trail — a common adaptive-reuse beat (dead rail corridor → trail) for villages/small towns that lost passenger/freight rail service.

## Residential pattern
Compact grid radiating from the courthouse square; land area under 1 sq mi land means farmland is reached within roughly 400-600 m of the town center in most directions.

## Traffic/zoning
County-seat status brings a modest non-residential cluster beyond the pure-village norm: courthouse/county offices, Stark County High School, and Stark County Junior High School are all located in town — i.e., even a village-tier community can host a small consolidated school system when it holds county-seat status, unlike similarly-sized non-seat villages (contrast with Elma, IA, which lost its school to consolidation despite similar population).
