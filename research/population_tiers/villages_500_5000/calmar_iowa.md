# Calmar, Iowa (Winneshiek County)

Population: 1,125 (2020 census). Land area 1.10 sq mi, 0 sq mi water. Computed density ≈ 1,023/sq mi. Coordinates 43°11′12″N 91°52′20″W, elevation 1,247 ft.

## Core layout
Platted 1854, grew when the Chicago, Milwaukee & St. Paul Railroad reached it in 1868, incorporated 1869. Calmar's core is organized around a **Public Square** and sits at the literal highway junction of US-52 with State Routes 24 and 150 (both state routes terminate in Calmar) — i.e., a genuine small-town crossroads-and-square layout rather than a pure rail-siding strip. The Calmar Passenger Depot (built 1915 by the CM&StP Railroad, replacing an earlier Depot Hotel destroyed by fire) stands about **one block north of the commercial district and Public Square** — a concrete, sourced distance figure: depot-to-square offset ≈ 1 block (roughly 90-120 m at typical block scale).

## Residential pattern
Small grid centered on the Public Square with highway frontage; large lots typical of the tier; farmland reached within a few hundred meters given total land area of only 1.10 sq mi.

## Traffic/zoning
Functions as a genuine regional traffic node despite its tiny size — three state/US highways meet here, which is atypical for a village this small (most highway-junction towns are larger).
