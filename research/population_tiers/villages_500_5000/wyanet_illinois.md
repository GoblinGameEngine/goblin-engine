# Wyanet, Illinois (Bureau County)

Population: 886 (2020 census), 871 (2022 est). Land area 0.946 sq mi. Density 937/sq mi. Elevation 653 ft.

## Core layout
Wyanet is unusual in having **two successive railroad-driven identity changes**: originally an 1821 trading-post settlement called "Center," it was platted in 1854, then renamed "Kingston" in 1853 when the Chicago Rock Island & Pacific first built through, then renamed again to "Wyanet" in 1855 when the Chicago, Burlington & Quincy completed a second line through town. Two rail lines crossing historically implies a compact downtown near their junction, similar in structural type to Hoopeston, IL (see that file) but at 1/5th the population — i.e., a village-scale version of the "two-railroad-crossing town" pattern rather than the city-scale version.
