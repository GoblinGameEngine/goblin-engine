# Boardwalks and piers

Photos: `remake/reference/COAST-boardwalk-*`, `COAST-*pier*`, `COAST-UKpier-*` (sources.json in each).

## Boardwalks

**Atlantic City (NJ)** -- the original. 1870: 8 ft wide, 1 mile, about 1 ft above the sand, built so
sand stayed out of the hotels and railway cars. Rebuilt wider (14 ft), then lifted onto pilings 5 ft
above the beach so tides wash under. Today (from the 1916 rebuild): **60 ft wide, ~6 miles**,
herringbone planking on a concrete-and-steel substructure. Hotels/casinos face it directly; piers
spring off it at intervals. ([JOAC](https://journalofantiques.com/digital-publications/joac-magazine/features/atlantic-city-boardwalk-wooden-walkway-sea/),
[HABS NJ-1459 data](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/nj/nj1400/nj1459/data/nj1459data.pdf),
[Atlantic County](https://www.atlanticcountynj.gov/government/government-information/history-of-atlantic-county/atlantic-city-s-first-boardwalk-june-1870))

**Ocean City (NJ)** -- wooden plank sections **12 ft wide x 42 ft long**, prefabricated and dropped
onto wooden pilings; the deck is bolted to a timber frame; stairs, railings, ADA ramps down to the
beach at street ends. ([Breaking AC 2025](https://breakingac.com/news/2025/nov/02/ocean-city-boardwalk-undergoing-42-million-facelif/),
[Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/philly/news/new_jersey/shore/a-rare-glimpse-at-shore-ocean-city-without-part-of-its-boardwalk-20171203.html))

**Wildwood (NJ)** -- ~2.5 miles / 38 blocks; amusement piers (Morey's) off it; tramcars since 1949.
**Seaside Heights** -- ~1 mile, Casino Pier amusement park (30+ rides) and a water park at the shore.
([Islands](https://www.islands.com/2239305/jersey-shore-hands-down-boardwalks-ocean-views-seaside-fun/),
[Visit NJ](https://visitnj.org/new-jerseys-iconic-boardwalks))

**Virginia Beach** -- a concrete oceanfront boardwalk ~40 blocks (1st-42nd St) between the beach and a
continuous hotel wall; Atlantic Ave one block back is the commercial street.
([Visit VB](https://www.visitvirginiabeach.com/listing/atlantic-avenue/557/))

**Hollywood (FL) "Broadwalk"** -- **2.5 miles, 30 ft wide**, paved (first 1923, pink cement), separate
lanes for walkers, bikes and joggers, bandshell, cafes and bars fronting it.
([APA Great Places](https://www.planning.org/greatplaces/spaces/2013/broadwalk.htm))

**Great Lakes (Grand Haven MI)** -- a riverside boardwalk along the harbour channel from downtown
to the beach and the south pier, lined with shops, food and the Coast Guard station.
([Visit Grand Haven](https://visitgrandhaven.com/things-to-do/attractions/lightouse-south-pier/))

### What a boardwalk carries (for the game)
Deck 8-60 ft wide (we'll use 12 m in cities, 6-8 m in villages), on pilings 1-2 m above the sand;
stairs/ramps to the beach at every street end; railings on the sea side; benches, lamp posts, trash
cans, shade pavilions, rain shelters; fronting it: arcades, pizza/fries/taffy stands, souvenir and
surf shops, ice cream, bars, hotels, a bandshell or music pavilion; lifeguard stands and dune
crossings on the sand below.

## Harrow Falls pier (Lake Tamsin) -- amenity programme
A ~330 m pier (between Naples/Pier 60 and Brighton), UK-style promenade pier with Florida fishing
amenities:
- **shore end**: a pavilion hall (Llandudno/Brighton type) with a cafe and arcade, pier gates and ticket kiosk
- **neck**: paired ornamental kiosks (sweets, souvenirs, ice cream, fries), benches with windbreaks, lamp standards
- **midpoint**: a covered shade pavilion (Pier 60), rod rental + bait & tackle shop (Naples), restrooms
- **head (T-platform)**: a bandstand (Brighton/Llandudno), a restaurant (Naples/Daytona), fishing rails all round,
  a landing stage for lake excursion boats (Eastbourne/Clevedon), a small lifeboat/rescue station (Southend)
- a boardwalk along the Harrow Falls lakefront connecting the pier foot to the Riverfront Promenade.
