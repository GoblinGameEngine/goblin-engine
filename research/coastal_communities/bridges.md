# Bridges for the expanded station -- contemporary (1970+) examples

The map's major crossings (tools/map_expanded.py's bridge scan): five over the **Kettle River**
(~500 m of water plus ~270 m of basin each side, ~1 km structures), two over the **Maumee**, three
over the **Miami** (road) plus the railway.  Photos: `remake/reference/BRIDGES/<example>/`.

## Examples researched
| Example | Type | Opened | Length / main span | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chesapeake Bay Bridge, westbound** (MD) | suspension | 1973 | 4.3 mi; main span 1,600 ft (488 m) | a suspension span almost exactly the Kettle's width |
| **Alfred Zampa Memorial (new Carquinez)** (CA) | suspension | 2003 | 1,056 m; main span 728 m | West Coast suspension, concrete towers |
| **Third Tacoma Narrows** (WA) | suspension | 2007 | main span 853 m | the newest US suspension bridge |
| **Seven Mile Bridge** (FL Keys) | precast segmental box-girder trestle | 1982 | 35,863 ft, 440 spans, 65 ft rise at the navigation hump | the classic long low trestle over water |
| **I-10 Twin Span** (Lake Pontchartrain, LA) | twin concrete trestles | 2009-11 | 5.4 mi; crest 80 ft | twin low trestles with a high-rise over the channel |
| **Atchafalaya Basin Bridge** (LA) | twin trestle over swamp | 1973 | 18 mi | a basin crossing: trestle on pile bents through wetland |
| **Manchac Swamp Bridge** (LA) | twin concrete trestle | 1979 | 22.8 mi | the same, over cypress swamp |
| **Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel parallel trestles** (VA) | trestle | 1999 | 17.6 mi complex | the 1999 parallel trestle |
| **Veterans' Glass City Skyway** (Toledo -- over the real **Maumee**) | cable-stayed, single pylon with glass top | 2007 | 8,800 ft over the river and its low banks | the Maumee's own contemporary crossing |
| **Long Beach International Gateway** (CA) | cable-stayed | 2020 | main span 1,000 ft | West Coast, contemporary |

## Assignment (one example per crossing, never reused) -- revised 2026-09-25 to match the coasts

Coast on the South Sea) now holds for the bridges too.  The Kettle's five come from Great Lakes ports;
the Maumee's mouth is East Coast; the Miami's are Californian.  Records: remake/catalog/XBR-*.json, XRR-01.json.

so nothing ever floats above the river bed or the basin.
