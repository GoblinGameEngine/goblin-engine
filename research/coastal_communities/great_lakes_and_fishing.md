# Great Lakes coastal communities, and fishing / cannery villages

Photos: `COAST-GreatLakes-*`, `COAST-cannery-*`, `COAST-fishing-Gloucester`.

## Great Lakes coastal communities
- **Grand Haven MI**: town on the Grand River where it meets Lake Michigan. A **boardwalk along the
  river channel** runs from downtown past shops, food and the **Coast Guard station** out to the
  beach (Grand Haven State Park) and the **south pier**: two red lights (the cylindrical inner light and
  the fog-house outer light on a big concrete foundation) joined to shore by an **elevated lighted
  catwalk** (1839 station). ([Grand Haven Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Haven_Light),
  [Visit Grand Haven](https://visitgrandhaven.com/things-to-do/attractions/lightouse-south-pier/),
  [Lighthouse Friends](https://www.lighthousefriends.com/light.asp?ID=189))
- **Saugatuck / Douglas MI**: on the Kalamazoo River as it winds through dunes to the lake; a
  natural harbour of calm water (marinas, boat tours, chain ferry); downtown of preserved 19th-century
  buildings (spared the fires and railroad sprawl); Oval Beach over the dunes. ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/chicago/travel/saugatuck-michigan),
  [travel-mi](https://www.travel-mi.com/is-south-haven-or-saugatuck-better.html))
- **South Haven MI**: white sand beaches, the pier lighthouse, a Maritime Museum with a tall ship.
  ([Michigan Beachtowns](https://www.michiganbeachtowns.com/south-haven/))

**Pattern**: a river mouth cut through dunes, **twin breakwater piers** with a lighthouse on the
end of one, a harbour/marina inside the river mouth, a downtown one to three blocks from the
harbour on a grid, cottages and inns up the bluff, a public beach on the lake side of the dunes,
a Coast Guard station at the channel. Zoning: waterfront/marine commercial along the channel,
central business district, resort residential on the bluffs, dune/park protection.

## Fishing and cannery villages
- **Lubec and Eastport, Maine**: twin centres of the US sardine industry, 1880s to the end of the 20th
  century. **Canneries, smokehouses, and lumber and coal sheds crowded onto wharves** along the
  downtown waterfront; plants ran day and night on the fish whistle; women packers. Eastport had 18
  factories by 1880. ([Lubec Maine Memory](https://lubec.mainememory.net/page/782/display.html),
  [Island Institute](https://www.islandinstitute.org/working-waterfront/a-sardine-packing-plants-20th-century-history/),
  [Smithsonian](https://ocean.si.edu/human-connections/history-cultures/nimble-fingers-women-behind-maines-canneries))
- **Astoria, Oregon** ("Cannery Capital of the World", 22 canneries): **canneries on timber pilings
  out over the water** -- long gable-roofed sheds, smokestacks, net lofts, the dock alongside; one pier
  600 ft into the Columbia. ([Astoria Wharf & Warehouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astoria_Wharf_and_Warehouse_Company),
  [Samuel Elmore Cannery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Elmore_Cannery))
- **Cannery Row, Monterey**: a continuous row along the waterfront street; each complex = **a large
  rectilinear cannery on the water side + a smaller warehouse inland, joined by an elevated enclosed
  conveyor "crossover" over the street** (16 at the peak). Hovden Cannery 1916, Monterey Canning Co.
  warehouse 1918. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannery_Row),
  [SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/CA-01-053-0015))
- **Gloucester, Massachusetts** (oldest US fishing port): wharves and processing plants line the
  harbour; unloading, net mending, ice, fuel, marine railways and boatyards. ([NOAA](https://coast.noaa.gov/digitalcoast/stories/gloucester.html),
  [NWWN case study](https://nationalworkingwaterfronts.com/portfolio_page/case-study-gloucester-massachusetts/))

**Our fishing village programme** (one per sea): a breakwater-sheltered harbour; 3-5 finger piers /
wharves on pilings with the fleet alongside; **the cannery** (long shed on pilings + inland warehouse
and a conveyor crossover, boiler house with a stack, can shop); an ice house, a fish market /
auction shed, net lofts and gear sheds, a fuel dock, a marine railway + boatyard, a
harbourmaster's office, a Coast Guard / life-saving station; a village of workers' houses up the slope,
a church, a general store, a diner, a bar.
