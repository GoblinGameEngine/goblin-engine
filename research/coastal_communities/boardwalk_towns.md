# Boardwalk communities: Jersey Shore, Florida, Charleston area, Virginia Beach

Photos: `remake/reference/COAST-boardwalk-*`, `COAST-doowop-motel-Wildwood`, `COAST-CasinoPier-SeasideHeights`,
`COAST-CapeMay-victorian`, `COAST-AsburyPark`, `COAST-VirginiaBeach-boardwalk`, `COAST-Hollywood-Broadwalk`, `COAST-FollyBeach`.

## Layout (the common pattern)
Barrier-island towns are laid out as a **grid parallel to the beach**:
- **Ocean City NJ**: numbered streets (1st-59th) run across the island from bay to ocean; named avenues
  (Bay, West, Central, Asbury, Wesley, Ocean) run along it. The boardwalk forms the ocean edge; the
  commercial spine (Asbury Ave) is the middle avenue; the bay side has marinas and bay houses. Founded
  around a central campground block. ([OCNJ circulation element](https://www.ocnj.us/media/KnightBuilding/PlanningZoning/OC_Circulation_Element_3-21-05.pdf),
  [OC Residential HD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean_City_Residential_Historic_District))
- Street order from the sea: **beach + dunes -> boardwalk -> beachfront row (hotels, arcades, food) ->
  the resort avenue (shops, bars) -> residential blocks of cottages -> the bay road / marinas.**
- Blocks are short across the island (~80-100 m) so every street reaches the beach; each street end
  gets a dune crossing and boardwalk stairs.

## Zoning (what goes where)
- **Virginia Beach Oceanfront Resort District** is a form-based code: a regulating plan of
  *frontages* (building types for each street), *street setbacks*, and a separate *height map*; public
  parking districts instead of lots per building; bars, drive-throughs and commercial parking are
  restricted to mapped streets; adjoining residential areas are protected with screening and
  setbacks. The RT-1 Resort Tourist district holds the oceanfront hotels.
  ([VB Planning](https://planning.virginiabeach.gov/zoning/form-based-code/oceanfront-resort-district),
  [VB zoning code](https://www.zoneomics.com/code/virginia-beach-VA/chapter_14))
- Jersey Shore towns typically zone: **boardwalk commercial / amusement** (the boardwalk frontage),
  **resort commercial** (the main avenue), **beach residential** (cottages, duplexes), **marine
  commercial** on the bay (marinas, bait, boat rental), and public beach (badge-controlled).
  Beach access is paid by **beach badges**, which fund lifeguards and upkeep.
  ([Beach tag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beach_tag))

## Buildings used
- **Hotels / motels**: Wildwood's 1950s-60s **doo-wop motels** (Googie: angled roofs, neon, plastic
  palms, kidney pools; NRHP "Motels of the Wildwoods") -- ([National Trust](https://savingplaces.org/stories/retro-roadmap-wildwood-motels-midcentury-modern-for-everybody),
  [Preservation NJ](https://preservationnj.org/10-most/the-wildwoods/)); 10-20-storey oceanfront hotels
  in Virginia Beach and Atlantic City.
- **Victorian cottages and inns** (Cape May: gingerbread, deep porches, towers).
- **Beach bungalows** (cedar shake, small, raised) and 1950s-60s "articulated boxes" -- ([Seebohm & Cook,
  Cottages and Mansions of the Jersey Shore](https://www.rutgersuniversitypress.org/cottages-and-mansions-of-the-jersey-shore/9780813540160)).
- **Boardwalk frontage**: arcades, pizza, fudge/taffy, fries, ice cream, surf and souvenir shops,
  mini golf, amusement piers (Casino Pier, Morey's), a convention hall/casino (Asbury Park).
- **Beach**: wooden or aluminium **lifeguard stands** (the Jersey Shore started ocean beach patrols),
  a beach patrol HQ, restrooms/showers, dune fencing with sanctioned crossings
  ([Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/philly/news/breaking/20100813_Traditions_change_as_Shore_towns_test_a_new_lifeguard_stand.html));
  old **US Life-Saving Service stations** repurposed ([NJ Monthly](https://njmonthly.com/articles/shore-travel/jersey-shores-historic-life-saving-service-stations-infused-with-new-purpose/)).

## Florida
- **Hollywood**: Broadwalk (2.5 mi, 30 ft) with bandshell; hotels and low condos behind; A1A is the
  beach road. ([APA](https://www.planning.org/greatplaces/spaces/2013/broadwalk.htm))
- **Daytona Beach**: Main Street Pier (1925), boardwalk amusements, a bandshell (coquina), motels on
  A1A. Florida piers anchor their towns: the pier sits at the end of the main street, with a plaza,
  parking and bait shops at its foot (Naples Pier at 12th Ave S, Pier 60 at the end of Causeway Blvd).

## Charleston-area beach towns
- **Folly Beach**: Center Street is the one commercial strip from the causeway to the beach, ending
  at the pier (with a tackle shop and shade); bohemian, surfer, raised beach houses on the side streets.
- **Isle of Palms**: county beach park with the fullest amenity set (restrooms, dressing rooms,
  showers, playground, volleyball, grills, snack bar, chair rental).
  ([Locating CHS](https://www.locatingchs.com/blog/folly-beach-vs-isle-of-palms-vs-sullivans-island-comparison),
  [Folly pier](https://follybeach.com/pier/))
