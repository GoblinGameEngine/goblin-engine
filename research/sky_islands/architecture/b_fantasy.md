# B4. Fantasy art and games: what each exaggerates that we can use

Each entry gives its source, what the work exaggerates, and the rule we take for the sky islands.

- **Ghibli, *Spirited Away*, Aburaya (the bathhouse)**
  - **Source:** modelled on Dōgo Onsen Honkan (1893), a "confectionery" of **layered roofs**, a white heron on the top
    turret, red interiors, and a town of red-lit eateries ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgo_Onsen);
    [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/travel/five-places-in-japan-that-look-like-scenes-from-spirited-away)).
  - **Exaggerates:** a tall stack of pagoda eaves on a narrow base; red and green paint; lanterns along a bridge; a
    sky-blue bathhouse mural.
  - **Our rule:** **"the bathhouse tower"**: 5–8 storeys of stacked eaves, each storey a little different. A red bridge leads
    to it, and the ground lane is lantern-lit.
- **Ghibli, *Howl's Moving Castle***
  - **Source:** the town is **Colmar** (Maison Pfister, 1537): half-timbered, coloured, turreted, along canals
    ([Outlook Traveller](https://www.outlooktraveller.com/destinations/international/did-you-know-about-this-french-town-that-inspired-miyazakis-howls-moving-castle)).
    The castle is a **heap of parts**: chimneys, turrets, a gun and shacks.
  - **Exaggerates:** additive clutter, with every add-on a different material.
  - **Our rule:** **"the junk castle"**: a core with bolted-on turrets, rooms and chimneys (vent stacks, as there is no
    flame), each one a different colour or material.
- **Ghibli, *Laputa***
  - **Exaggerates:** a **floating island** with a **giant tree**, roots wrapped round the ruins, **stepped terraces of
    arcades** and a dome; overgrown stone. The Zelda *Tears of the Kingdom* sky islands are often compared to it
    ([Zelda Universe](https://zeldauniverse.net/features/more-studio-ghibli-references-to-be-found-in-the-latest-tears-of-the-kingdom-trailers/)).
  - **Our rule:** **"the garden terraces"**: circular stepped arcades with moss and vines spilling over, and a green crown.
- **Ghibli, *Porco Rosso***
  - **Exaggerates:** Adriatic seaplane hotels; a red, white and green hotel on the water; wooden hangars.
  - **Our rule:** a **"hangar quarter"**: lark hangars with big sliding doors and hand-painted signs.
- **Zelda, *Tears of the Kingdom*, sky islands**
  - **Source:** Zonai motifs: **stone dragon heads, inverted pillars that narrow at the base**, lotus lamps, and a preference
    for **circles** ("an eternal bond") ([Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Circular_Design)).
  - **Our rule:** **round motifs** (round doors and porthole windows), and inverted-taper columns for a few "old Hundred"
    islands built by the Steward.
- ***BioShock Infinite*, Columbia** (a floating city)
  - **Source:** **1893 Columbian Exposition** Beaux-Arts in bright colonial brick and clapboard. Buildings **float on giant
    airbags and propellers**, and **different sections float at slightly different heights** in an uneven, cluttered
    skyline ([nettosgameroom](https://www.nettosgameroom.com/2025/02/bioshock-infinites-columbia-world-tour.html?m=1);
    [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/2014/05/02/bioshock-infinite-columbia-minecraft/);
    [WhatCulture](https://whatculture.com/gaming/12-stunning-video-game-locations-you-must-visit-before-you-die?page=6)).
    The design rule was "no geometry for geometry's sake: build it as if it were an actual city"
    ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2011-06-10-lead-artist-shawn-robertson-talks-the-aesthetic-evolution-of-bio.html)).
  - **Exaggerates:** fair-ground grandeur: banners, bunting, gilt lettering, ads everywhere.
  - **Our rule:** **"the exposition"**: one island style of white columns, gilt signs, bunting, carnival bulbs and fairground
    promenades. It is our nearest cousin, so take its signage, not its politics.
- **Final Fantasy IX, Lindblum**
  - **Source:** a castle town **inside the walls**, from French and German castle towns; the airships are a
    **Versailles château on top and a mahogany barge below**
    ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/creating-the-concept-of-the-osiris-and-airships-in-final-fantasy-ix/)).
  - **Our rule:** **two design languages in one building**, a fine top on a crude hull: a fine pavilion on a rough
    timber-and-rope base.
- **Final Fantasy VII, Midgar**
  - **Source:** the team wanted **"jumbled, eclectic, fun"**, from Tokyo, with the Wall Market from **Kabukichō**. The slums
    blend a Wild West town with favelas, with **exposed red girders and banners**
    ([Square Enix](https://www.square-enix-games.com/news/final-fantasy-vii-remake-designing-midgar);
    [Domus](https://domusweb.it/en/architecture/gallery/2020/04/20/midgar-a-dieselpunk-utopia-where-the-surroundings-determine-the-individuals.html)).
  - **Our rule:** **"the market under girders"**: exposed red structure, junk-built shops and banners.
- ***Blade Runner***
  - **Source:** Ridley Scott's "**retrofit**": as costs rise you add systems to the **outside** of old buildings instead of
    rebuilding, which gives a textured city. Syd Mead's elevated network leaves the ground "an access corridor"
    ([American Cinematographer](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/movies/blade-runner-1982-production-design-and-photography-american-cinematographer/)).
  - **Our rule:** **retrofit layers**: ducts, pipes, cable trays, AC boxes and **video and dot-matrix boards bolted on** over
    an older facade. Rain-wet reflections.
- ***Ghost in the Shell* (1995)**
  - **Exaggerates:** its city is modelled on **Hong Kong**: canals, dense signs in hanzi, haze *(general knowledge)*.
  - **Our rule:** **layered sign canyons** over water (our ponds and lakes).
- ***Hollow Knight*, City of Tears**
  - **Source:** a city in a cavern under a lake, in **perpetual rain**, of **tall towers with huge windows**; rich districts
    in **red and purple**, the poor in **blue** ([Hollow Knight wiki](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/City_of_Tears)). Victorian
    lamp posts and rails signal wealth ([Critical Play](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2025/05/16/critical-play-worldbuilding-dorian-gulley)).
  - **Our rule:** **"the lamp city"**: slim towers of tall arched windows, one cold-blue palette with warm lamps; snow, not
    rain.
- ***Disco Elysium*, Martinaise**
  - **Exaggerates:** a run-down harbour district of oil-painted, colour-blocked facades, failed commerce and faded signs
    *(general knowledge)*.
  - **Our rule:** **painted signs fading**, ghost signs on walls, and one or two failed shops on each poor island.
- ***Planescape*, Sigil** (the City of Doors)
  - **Exaggerates:** a ring-shaped city on the inside of a torus with no horizon, **knife-blade spikes** and **doors to
    everywhere** *(general knowledge)*.
  - **Our rule:** our station is already a ring. Take the **doorway arches as portals**: decorated "bridge gates" on every
    dock face, one design per clan.
- **Discworld, Ankh-Morpork**
  - **Exaggerates:** guilds, a shop for everything, punning shop names and comic overcrowding *(general knowledge)*.
  - **Our rule:** **shop-name humour and guild signs** (see b_signs_and_light.md).
- **Kowloon-inspired levels** (*Stray*, *Shenmue II*, *Call of Duty: Black Ops*)
  - **Exaggerates:** Kowloon reduced to **verticality, wet alleys, neon, AC drip, pipes and caged windows**; the player looks
    up through slots of sky *(general knowledge)*.
  - **Our rule:** the warren styles (b_warrens.md).

## What the islands should feel like
Combining the above, here is what the sky islands should be (and what the floor towns are not):
1. **Vertical and fused.** Every island is a single mass of buildings with slots and bridges, not a grid of detached houses.
2. **Additive and patched.** Every structure shows its add-ons. Weight is precious, so the add-ons are light: skycloth,
   corrugated sheet, timber, rope.
3. **Commercial loud.** Signs on every face and every scale, from noren to giant 3D figures and balloon-sized ads.
4. **Night is the show.** Several layers of light (b_signs_and_light.md §3). From the floor, an island at night is a
   **lantern in the sky**, with its balloons lit from inside.
5. **One strong motif per island** (a clan identity): round doors, stacked eaves, chevron bands, bells.
