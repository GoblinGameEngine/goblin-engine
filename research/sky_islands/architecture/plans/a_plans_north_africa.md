# North African warren plans and sections, as generator data (stream A)

Same conventions as a_plans.md (metres; every plan ends at a roof access). Values marked ≈ are estimates.

## A-P10 Ghorfa ksar (Ksar Ouled Soltane) — `ghorfa_court`
```
court: rectangle 18x28 .. 30x40 (one or two courts joined by a passage); paved; a well/pond in the middle
cell (ghorfa): 2.8 wide x 6.0 deep x 2.4 high, barrel-vaulted (semicircle on the 2.8 width), one door 0.9x1.6 + a
               small vent over it; party walls 0.4
stacking: rings of cells round the court, 4-6 tiers; tier height 2.6 (vault crown + 0.2 floor)
access: NO internal stairs: external stairs (0.7 wide, steep 50 deg) against the court walls every 4-6 cells,
        protruding beam-steps (0.25 out, every 0.35 up) between, plank landings 0.9 deep along tiers 2..n
roof: the top tier's vaults are the roof (walkable along a 1.2 m plank walk on the outer edge); a gate tower over
      the entrance with the stair head
outer face: blind (the ksar wall) except for the gate; in the sky version the outer face also carries cells
            facing the island's lanes (shops) on tiers 0-1
uses (sky): tier 0-1 shops/workshops (each cell = 1 shop, sign over the door), tiers 2+ stores & one-room homes
```

## A-P11 Tighremt (Dades / Aït Benhaddou kasbah) — `tighremt`
```
footprint: square 12x12 .. 18x18; corner towers 3x3 .. 4x4 rising 1-2 storeys above the body, tapering 4-6% per side
storeys: 3-5 (+1-2 in the towers); storey 3.0
court: central 3x3 .. 5x5 light court open to the sky (or a roofed court with a chebek opening), arcaded corridors
floors: 0 stores / workshops (blind outer walls except one door) ; 1 stores / kitchen ; 2 winter home ; 3+ homes ;
        roof terrace between the towers = summer home, garden, lark cradle
towers: each tower holds a stair (one is the main stair from the ground to the roof) -> roof access in every tower
decoration: the top 1/3 of the towers and the parapet band: geometric relief (chevrons, lozenges, saw-tooth) cut
            into the render; small windows in the top storeys only
```

## A-P12 Ghardaïa cone (M'zab ksar) — `hill_cone`
```
site: a hill (rolling relief) -> rings of terraced houses following contours, each ring 1 house deep (8-12 m),
      ring lanes 2-3 wide, radial stepped lanes every 30-40 m
house: 8x10 .. 12x14, 2-3 storeys, a covered central court with a chebek grille in the roof, an arcaded loggia on the
       roof terrace (2-3 arches) facing outward; walls hand-irregular (rounded corners, no straight edges)
top: the mosque/hall with a truncated pyramidal minaret (base 4x4, top 2x2, 18-25 m) = the island crown & sign mast
foot: the rahba: an arcaded rectangular market square 20x30 near the bridges, shops in the arcade
```

## A-P13 Matmata pit house — `pit_court`
```
pit: circle d 8..14, depth 6..9 below the deck (needs rock: the islands' skystone is 28-100 m deep), with a pond/well
rooms: carved round the pit wall on 1-2 levels: each 3.0 wide x 5..8 deep x 2.6 high, vaulted; upper-level rooms
       reached by a rope-and-niche stair or a carved ramp; the pit's rim on the deck has a low parapet and a canopy
access: a sloped tunnel 2.0 wide from the deck down into the pit (the entrance), a second tunnel to a neighbouring pit
above: the deck above the rooms is a garden or a light building (the pit reads from the air as a glowing round hole)
```

## A-P14 Medina warren (Fez / Tunis / Cairo haret) — `medina_warren`
```
grain: courtyard houses 8x10 .. 16x16, 3-5 storeys, sharing party walls; lanes 1.2-3 wide, dead ends
sabat: every 20-40 m of lane a room bridges it at storey 1-2 (the lane passes under, 2.6 clear)
souk: a vaulted covered street 4 wide through the ground floors of a row: shop cells 2.5 wide x 3 deep both sides,
      a skylight in every 4th vault bay
upper: mashrabiyas/jetties overhang the lanes so the upper storeys nearly meet (0.6-1.0 gap at the top)
roofs: terraces at mixed heights joined by short ladders and planks; pigeon towers; laundry; the minaret/sign mast
```
