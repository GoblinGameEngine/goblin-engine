# Plan B-YK: the yokocho alley of micro-bars (Golden Gai / Omoide Yokocho)

```
block:         a cluster 20-40 x 15-30 cut by 2-3 alleys 1.2-1.8 wide (one-person passages 0.8 between)
units:         alley_bar buildings 2.8-4 wide x 4-5 deep, 2 storeys (+ optional third), shared walls
ground:        a counter bar of 4-8 seats; glass/wood sliding door + noren; a red chochin over the door
upper:         a second bar or a room reached by an external steep stair (stair_external, 0.6 wide) or inside
roofs:         low pitched or flat with ac units, a tangle of wires (overhead_wires) across the alleys
lights:        lantern_string across the alley every 3-4 m; chochin pair per door; small neon_script per bar
               (each bar's own name); painted_board menus
```
