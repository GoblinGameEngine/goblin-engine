# Plan B-CC: the capsule (skybox) stack on a core

```
core:          a square core 5 x 5 (stair + lift + services), 8-14 storeys, structure only
capsules:      boxes 2.5 x 2.5 x 4.0, bolted on 4 faces of the core, 2-4 per face per storey, each turned
               0/90 deg or offset half a box; 1-3 capsules may be missing (a gap - "away on a hoist")
window:        one porthole 1.0-1.3 dia at the outer end (porthole_window)
access:        a short gangway from the core landing to each capsule door
variety:       capsules are repainted per owner (clan colour palette) - the stack is a patchwork
bridges:       skybridges between cores at every 3rd storey
night:         porthole_glow rings in the mood colour; the core's stair glass lit
```
