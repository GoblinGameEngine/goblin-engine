# Plan B-ZK: the zakkyo signboard building

```
lot:           5-9 wide x 10-16 deep; often a corner
storeys:       6-10 (+0-1 basement not modelled); storey 3.0-3.2 (commercial)
core:          at the back: one small lift (1.4 x 1.4) + a stair (1.1 wide); landings 2.5 deep
floors:        ONE TENANT PER FLOOR (bar, karaoke, clinic, tea house, tailor, pawn, office), each floor a different
               window treatment (tenants fit out their own); 30% of floors get a floor-to-floor glass front
ground:        entrance + directory_board (one row per tenant), a shop or a stand-up eatery, vending_glow
front:         sign_vertical_stack up the street corner: one lightbox panel (0.6 x 1) per floor; dot_matrix band
               over the entrance; noren in the ground-floor shop door
roof:          rooftop_billboard (40%) or roof_shrine (20%) or water tank; roof door from the stair
```
