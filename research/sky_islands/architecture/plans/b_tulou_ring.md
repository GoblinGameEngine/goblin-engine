# Plan B-TU: the ring house (Hakka tulou) as an Aloft clan hall

```
plan:          a ring, outer diameter 30-62, ring depth 6-8 (rooms) + gallery 1.5 facing the court
storeys:       3-5; storey 2.8
rooms:         3-4 per 10 m of ring per floor; 1 per family per floor (sectors), stair in every 8th sector
floors:        G: kitchens + long kitchen hall (canon: every meal together); 1F: stores (grain, wool, skybox
               goods); 2F+: bedrooms
court:         open; inner ring of 1-2 storeys (school / library / workshops); centre: shrine or bell tower
               (bell_tower) and the clan's well (a cloud-net tank)
entry:         one or two gates; the outer wall nearly blind at G-1F, windows above
roof:          a continuous pitched ring roof (eave_bulbs at night) or a flat ring terrace with a rail
night:         every gallery lit with lantern_cn rows - the ring glows from inside
```
