# A4. Mumbai

Research stream A, 2026-10-14. Values marked ≈ are typical estimates.

## Chawls: the gallery tenement
- **Plan:**
  - rows of single rooms off a **long common gallery or corridor**, with **shared toilets at the ends** of each floor;
  - ≈ **20 tenements per floor** (BDD chawls);
  - rooms ≈ 160 sq ft (≈ 3.0 × 5.0 m; the "10 × 12 ft" room is ≈ 3.0 × 3.7 m);
  - 3–4 storeys; galleries ≈ 1.5–2 m deep.
  [citizenmatters BDD](https://citizenmatters.in/is-the-bdd-chawl-redevelopment-mumbais-chance-for-course-correction-in-urban-planning-44802), [The Book Review: Through the prism of a structure](https://www.thebookreviewindia.org/through-the-prism-of-a-structure/), [MHADA Naigaon](https://mhada.gov.in/sites/default/files/bddchal/pdf/naigaonppt.pdf)
- **The gallery is the living room:** storage, laundry, social life by day, sleeping at night. Railings are hung with
  drying clothes; doors stand open; festival lanterns hang along it.
- **BDD chawls (1920s):** concrete, 4 storeys, long slab blocks; built to house mill workers.
- **For us:**
  - the **gallery block** is the cheapest dense housing type and maps cleanly to the shared kit: one repeated
    room-and-gallery bay;
  - at night, a **string of lit doors and lanterns along every gallery**.

## Art Deco (the Oval and Marine Drive; UNESCO 2018)
- **Context:** 94 buildings round the Oval Maidan and along Marine Drive (1930s–40s), built on Backbay reclamation;
  5–7 storeys, reinforced concrete, rendered in pastels.
  [UNESCO doc 168687](https://whc.unesco.org/document/168687), [India Seminar](https://www.india-seminar.com/2019/722/722_abha_narain_lambah.htm), [Google Arts](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/8QUxI29tmcJjKg)
- **Vocabulary:**
  - **curved corner balconies** and curved verandahs;
  - **porthole windows**, nautical streamlining;
  - **sunburst** and wave motifs, flora and fauna motifs;
  - **zig-zag turrets** and ziggurat crowns on corner plots (Eros and Metro cinemas; Soona Mahal's red bands);
  - **"flying saucer" terraces** (Chateau Marine, Zaver Mahal);
  - geometric grilles; the Frozen Fountain motif;
  - **distinctive building-name lettering** in relief.
- **Eyebrows:** thin horizontal sunshade bands over the windows (≈ 0.4–0.6 m deep, common in Bombay Deco; the term
  itself wasn't confirmed by a source).
- **Palette:** cream, pink, mint and ochre (≈ #efe1c6, #e8b9a8, #b8d8c2, #d8b067), with white bands and dark green
  or rust trims.
- **Night:** Marine Drive is the "**Queen's Necklace**", an arc of streetlights. Name lettering lit by neon;
  rooftop advertising hoardings on the Deco blocks.

## Indo-Saracenic and Bombay Gothic
- **Vocabulary:**
  - onion domes, **cusped arches**, **chhatris**, jalis and overhanging eaves (chhajja);
  - pinnacles and minarets over Gothic or classical planning.
  [Architecture of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture_of_Mumbai), [GKToday](https://www.gktoday.in/indo-saracenic-style/)
- **Examples:**
  - **CSMT** (Victoria Terminus): Venetian Gothic with a central dome, polychrome stone and gargoyles;
  - Prince of Wales Museum and the Gateway of India (Wittet);
  - Rajabai Tower.
- **Palette:** buff basalt and yellow Kurla stone (≈ #c8b48a), blue-grey basalt (≈ #6a6e72), red dressings
  (≈ #9c4a36), white domes.
- **Night:** CSMT's facade is projection-mapped and floodlit in changing colours, the precedent for **uplit,
  colour-cycling civic facades**.

## Dharavi: the incremental stack (the tool-house)
- The **"tool-house"**: the home is the workshop. Ground floor is shop or workshop, with living above; buildings
  grow upward storey by storey (G+1 to G+4).
  [urbz: Resilience and recognition of an unseen typology](https://www.urbz.net/articles/resilience-and-recognition-unseen-typology-part-1), [urbz: The Way Up](https://urbz.net/node/767), [EPFL Homegrown Workshop](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lasur/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Homegrown_Workshop.pdf)
- **Mild-steel external stairs** are added when a storey is added, placed ad hoc on the street.
- **Upper storeys:**
  - each upper storey is **slightly different**: a different material, colour or setback, and often cantilevered
    0.3–0.6 m over the one below;
  - blue tarpaulins, corrugated sheet roofs, water tanks (black plastic Sintex drums), and dish antennas.
- **Districts vary by trade:** potters at Kumbharwada (kilns and courtyards), leather, recycling, tailoring.
- **For us:** this is **the stacked-warren generator rule**: a base volume, then upper volumes each offset,
  re-coloured and re-materialed; a bolt-on steel stair per level; clutter on every roof.

## Khotachiwadi (Girgaum): Konkan-Portuguese cottages
- Wooden houses of 2–3 storeys with **large overhanging first-floor balconies**, **external teak (often spiral)
  staircases**, open verandahs and latticed stairs.
  [Incredible India](https://incredibleindia.gov.in/en/maharashtra/mumbai/preserving-goan-heritage-in-mumbai-khotachiwadi), [The Decor Journal](https://www.thedecorjournalindia.com/khotachiwadi-portuguese-village-mumbai)
- **Palette:** blue, ochre, peach, green, red (≈ #3f7fb5, #d6a24a, #f0b49a, #5c9a5a, #b8432f).
- **Status:** 65 houses in the past, 28 now. At Christmas: star lanterns and fairy lights on every balcony.

## Mohammed Ali Road and Bhendi Bazaar (the night market)
- During Ramadan the streets round Minara Masjid are **lit with "so many lights and festoons"**, and food stalls run
  until suhoor. Out of Ramadan the street is open till about 2 am.
  [NPR/Michigan Public 2020](https://www.michiganpublic.org/2020-04-30/mumbais-historic-ramadan-food-bazaar-closes-for-the-first-time-due-to-coronavirus), [TableJourney](https://tablejourney.com/neighborhood/mumbai/mohammed-ali-road/)
- **Bhendi Bazaar's Bohri buildings:** 4–5 storeys of timber-framed balconies (cast-iron and wooden fretwork)
  over shops.
- **Night:** green and white bulb festoons over the street in catenaries, lit minarets, and grills glowing over
  pavements.
- **For us:** a "festoon canopy" over market lanes: **lights_** anchors on facing facades, strung across.

## Irani cafés
- Corner cafés with bentwood chairs, marble tables, mirrors, high ceilings with fans, **glass-front display cabinets
  and enamel signboards** listing house rules.
- **For us:** the "café_corner" ground-floor fit-out, with a period lettered sign.
