# Straits shophouses (Penang, Malacca, Singapore)

**Mark:** Arcaded rows of two-three storey shophouses with a continuous covered five-foot way, pastel stucco and Peranakan tiles, timber shutters and pintu pagar half-doors, Chinese temples and mosques side by side.

Sources: George Town, Penang; Malacca Heeren St / Jonker; Singapore Chinatown, Emerald Hill, Kampong Glam

## How it grew
- Early (1790s-1850s): 1-2 storey plain, clay tiles, timber windows.
- Southern Chinese / Early Straits Eclectic (1840s-1900s): 2 storeys, carved timber, vents.
- Late Straits Eclectic (1900s-1940): 3 storeys, Peranakan tiles, pilasters, French windows, riot of colour.
- Art Deco (1930s-60s): geometric fronts, flagpoles, simpler.

## Generator grammar
- **Cell:** 5.0 x 3.5 m; storey 3.6 m; 2-4 storeys.
- **Walls:** lime-plastered brick, painted pastels; glazed Peranakan tile dados; timber jack roofs; 0.3 m thick; palette #f2d7a8, #a8d0c8, #f0b8b0, #c8d8a8, #e8e2d4, #4a6a8a, #2f7a5a; weathering: mould on plaster, faded pastels, cracked tiles.
- **Mass:** Long terraces (40-100 m) of 5 m x 20-40 m shophouses back to back, with an air well halfway; a continuous covered walkway; temples and clan houses inserted.
- **Streets:**
  - main street: 12 m (two arcades facing)
  - five-foot way: 1.8 m (covered, tiled, continuous; public)
  - back lane: 3 m (service)
  - lorong: 4 m (side street)
- **Growth, in layers:**
  - early: low shophouses [brick, clay tile, timber]
  - eclectic: 3-storey ornate fronts [stucco, tiles, timber]
  - overgrowth: rooftop additions, sheds over air wells, AC, signs [zinc, aluminium]
  - cyberpunk: neon calligraphy over the arcade, LED pilasters, hawker centre glow [LED]
- **Openings:** Pairs or triplets of full-height timber casements with louvres per upper bay; pintu pagar; bat-motif vents.
- **Roofs:** Pitched clay (Marseilles) tile, gable firewalls between units; jack roofs.
- **Ornament:** Pilasters, Peranakan tiles, plaster festoons, Chinese name plaques, Malay timber fretwork.
- **Public spaces:** Clan houses, temple squares, mosque courtyards, hawker centres; on an island: a hawker square under rain trees, a temple court, a harbour/pond promenade.
- **Water:** The harbour edge, monsoon drains.
- **Street hierarchy:** main street 12 m with arcades > lorong 4 m > back lane 3 m

## Foliage
- rain tree (Samanea saman): huge umbrella, 15-25 m, squares, avenues (#3f6a35)
- frangipani (Plumeria): small candelabra tree, white flowers, 3-6 m, temples, courts (#f2efe0)
- palms (coconut, areca): palm, 5-15 m, edges (#5a8a3a)
- potted plants on the five-foot way: pots, 0.3-1.5 m, arcades (#4f7f3d)

## Cyberpunk overlay
- neon calligraphy banners over the arcades
- LED-lit pilasters
- lit hawker stalls
- projection on pastel fronts
- **Night:** Arcades glowing like lantern tunnels, pastel fronts uplit, hawker centre steam and white strip light.

- proportions: 5 m bays, 2-3 storeys, tall storeys 3.6 m
- rhythm: perfectly regular bays
- depth: arcade 1.8 m deep

- materials: plaster, tile, timber
