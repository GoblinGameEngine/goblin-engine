# Manila: Binondo and the estero settlements (Tondo)

**Mark:** Stone ground floors with timber upper storeys overhanging the street, capiz-shell sliding windows and ventanillas, Chinese arches of Binondo -- and beyond them dense stilt shanties of scrap over the esteros, jeepney colours everywhere.

Sources: Binondo (Chinatown); Intramuros / Vigan bahay na bato; Tondo informal settlements; esteros

## How it grew
- Bahay kubo (stilt hut of bamboo and nipa) -> bahay na bato (stone below, timber above, capiz windows).
- Binondo: Chinese merchants' shophouses with arches, estero canals, a church plaza.
- 20th c.: informal settlements of salvaged wood, plywood and galvanized iron on stilts along the esteros; jeepneys painted chrome and colour.

## Generator grammar
- **Cell:** 4.0 x 3.5 m; storey 3.0 m; 2-4 storeys.
- **Walls:** adobe/stone ground floor, timber upper floor; shanties of plywood and galvanized iron; 0.4 m thick; palette #b8a48a, #6b4a2e, #e8e0c8, #9a9a90, #d84a2a, #2a7ab8, #e8c040; weathering: moss on stone, rust on GI sheets.
- **Mass:** Old core of 2-3 storey bahay na bato blocks round a church plaza with arcaded Chinese shophouses; edges of dense stilt shanties along canals, 1-3 storeys, packed.
- **Streets:**
  - plaza street: 12 m (jeepneys)
  - calle: 7 m (overhanging upper floors)
  - eskinita (alley): 1.2 m (through the shanties, planks over water)
  - estero (canal): 8 m (stilt houses on both banks)
- **Growth, in layers:**
  - stone and timber: bahay na bato and shophouses [adobe, timber, capiz]
  - shanties: stilt shacks over the esteros [plywood, GI sheet, bamboo]
  - overgrowth: extra storeys of GI, bridges, wires [GI, cable]
  - cyberpunk: jeepney-chrome LED, karaoke neon, sari-sari shop signs [LED]
- **Openings:** Capiz-shell sliding window panels, ventanillas with balusters below, wide eaves.
- **Roofs:** Hipped tile or GI roofs with deep eaves; shanties flat GI held with tyres and stones.
- **Ornament:** Chinese arches (Binondo), church baroque, jeepney art.
- **Public spaces:** Church plaza, a market, basketball courts everywhere; on an island: a plaza with a church/temple, a basketball court in every quarter, a canal promenade, a mangrove pond.
- **Water:** Esteros; a mangrove pond.
- **Street hierarchy:** plaza street 12 m > calles 7 m > esteros 8 m > eskinitas 1.2 m
