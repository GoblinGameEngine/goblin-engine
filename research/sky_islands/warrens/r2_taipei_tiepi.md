# Taipei alleys and the iron-sheet rooftop layer (tiepiwu)

**Mark:** Four-to-six storey tiled walk-ups whose every window wears a steel cage and every roof a second house of corrugated iron sheet; arcaded ground floors (qilou) full of scooters and night-market stalls.

Sources: Taipei old districts (Wanhua, Datong); rooftop additions (頂樓加蓋); night markets

## How it grew
- Japanese era and later: qilou arcaded shophouses (tingzijiao, covered walkways).
- 1960s-80s: 4-6 storey apartment walk-ups clad in small ceramic tiles, iron window cages (tiechuang).
- 1980s-: almost every roof gets an illegal corrugated iron-sheet addition; balconies enclosed; plants everywhere.

## Generator grammar
- **Cell:** 5.0 x 3.2 m; storey 3.2 m; 4-7 storeys.
- **Walls:** concrete clad in small ceramic tiles (white, beige, pink, brown); iron-sheet rooftops in blue/green/rust; 0.2 m thick; palette #e8e2d8, #d8c8b0, #c8a890, #5a8aa8, #4a8a6a, #a85a3a; weathering: black streaks under cages, rust.
- **Mass:** Blocks of 4-6 storey walk-ups with arcaded ground floors, each topped by a 1-2 storey iron-sheet house; narrow alleys (xiang, nong) between.
- **Streets:**
  - road with qilou arcades: 12 m (scooters parked in the arcade)
  - lane (xiang): 6 m
  - alley (nong): 3 m (potted plants, scooters)
  - night market street: 8 m (stalls down the middle)
- **Growth, in layers:**
  - arcades: qilou shophouses [brick, concrete]
  - walk-ups: tiled 4-6 storeys with iron cages [tile, iron]
  - rooftop: iron-sheet houses on every roof, water tanks, gardens [corrugated iron]
  - cyberpunk: night-market LED, temple neon, convenience-store white [LED]
- **Openings:** Aluminium windows behind projecting iron cages full of plants and laundry.
- **Roofs:** Flat concrete with iron-sheet structures and stainless water towers.
- **Ornament:** Temple roofs (dragons, cut porcelain), signs.
- **Public spaces:** A temple square with a night market, a small park; on an island: a temple plaza night market, a pond park, an arcaded main road.
- **Water:** Tanks; a pond.
- **Street hierarchy:** arcaded road 12 m > night-market street 8 m > lane 6 m > alley 3 m
