# R3: Tyrrhenian canyon towns (Naples, Genoa, Palermo)

**Island culture:** a vertical port city of narrow, sunless lanes cut between six- to eight-storey palazzi. The street is the living room: ground-floor one-room homes (*bassi*) open straight onto the lane, laundry crosses overhead, and votive shrines are set into the walls. The city's mark is the canyon, roughly 10:1 height to width. It is a dense, walkable, noisy, sacred and profane city.

## Examples
- Naples, Quartieri Spagnoli: a 16th-century garrison grid of about 18×12 lanes on 800,000 m², about 14,000 residents. The lanes are so narrow that sun rarely reaches the street. Bassi, laundry, the Maradona mural on Via Emanuele De Deo, and the Cyop&Kaf murals. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartieri_Spagnoli), [summerinitaly](https://www.summerinitaly.com/guide/the-spanish-quarter), [italia.it murals](https://www.italia.it/en/campania/naples/things-to-do/naples-itinerary-murals-and-street-art)
- Naples: *edicole votive*, the neighbourhood altars in Quartieri Spagnoli, Sanità, Forcella and Pendino. [dmo-napoli](https://dmo-napoli.inera.it/en/article/votive-shrines-and-neighbourhood-altars-the-sacred-soul-of-the-alleys/), [naplesldm](https://naplesldm.com/votiveshrine.php)
- Naples stone: Neapolitan Yellow Tuff (*tufo giallo*), used since the Greek foundation, and the city's only natural building stone. It is paired with grey *piperno*, and that pairing gives the city its look. The ground is honeycombed with tuff quarries. [virtualexplorer PDF](https://virtualexplorer.com.au/system/files/papers/00261/assets/urban-geology-in-the-neapolitan-area.pdf), [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4605/5/1/7)
- Genoa, the *caruggi*: one of Europe's largest medieval centres. Some lanes are barely a metre wide between six-storey buildings, with windows almost touching. Slate roofs, sculpted portals, pastel and trompe-l'oeil facades. [italia.it](https://www.italia.it/en/liguria/genoa/citta-vecchia), [wonderfulitaly](https://wonderfulitaly.eu/en/magazine/arte-e-cultura/the-carruggi-of-genova-3527), [visitgenoa](https://www.visitgenoa.it/en/node/4094)
- Palermo, Albergheria and Ballarò: baroque grandeur beside collapse. Satellite dishes sprout from decaying baroque facades, around the market. [italysegreta](https://italysegreta.com/it/attraverso-lobiettivo-di-tommaso-serra-larchivio-dellabbandono-dellalbergheria/), [lonelyplanet.fr](https://www.lonelyplanet.fr/destinations/europe/italie/palerme/albergheria-et-ballaro), [Polimi on Ballarò](https://re.public.polimi.it/handle/11311/1170843)

## Generator grammar
- **Cell:** 4.5 m bays. Storeys are 4.2 m at the ground floor (vaulted bassi) and 3.4–3.8 m above. Height is 5–8 storeys, with lanes 1.5–4 m wide (height-to-width about 6–10:1).
- **Walls:** 0.6–0.9 m tuff or rubble masonry, lime-rendered and painted.
  - Palette: tuff yellow `#c9a85c`, ochre `#d4a05a`, Pompeii red `#a8432f`, faded pink `#d9a3a0`, Genoese pastel `#e6c48a` and `#c87e5a`, grey trim `#5e6166` (piperno or slate), shutter green `#3f5d3a`.
  - Weathering: heavy. Damp-stained bases (darker bands 0–1.5 m), flaking render patches showing tuff, black streaks under sills.
- **Mass:** perimeter blocks around small courts (*cortili*), entered through a big arched carriage portal (*portone*). Stair halls open to the court. Blocks fill their plots completely, and the lane is the void.
- **Streets:** a straight grid (Spanish quarter) or bent medieval lanes (Genoa). Steps (*salite*, gradoni) climb across the slope. Arches and flying buttress-beams span the lanes. Each lane has a 0.15 m gutter and basalt (*basoli*) paving `#3b3a38`.
- **Growth:**
  - Layer 0: Greek or Roman foundations and tuff vaults, ground-level bassi.
  - Layer 1: Renaissance and baroque palazzi (cornices, portals, wrought-iron balconies).
  - Layer 2: 19th-century raised storeys (attics, rooftop *superfetazioni* in brick).
  - Layer 3: 20th-century tin and concrete rooftop rooms and closed-in loggias.
  - Layer 4: cyberpunk.
- **Openings:** tall French windows (1.1×2.6 m) with shallow iron balconies (0.4–0.6 m deep) on every upper window, and green louvred shutters. The bassi have a full-width glazed double door at the ground floor.
- **Roofs:** flat terraces (*lastrico*) with parapets, rooftop laundry, water tanks and pergolas. Genoa has slate hip roofs.
- **Ornament:** stucco cornices and window aedicules, painted quoins and trompe-l'oeil (Genoa), majolica shrine panels.
- **Public space:** one *largo* or piazzetta at each lane junction (6–15 m), a market lane (Ballarò) with awnings, church fronts with steps.
- **Water:** a fountain in the court, cisterns underground.

## Street hierarchy
- **Main street (*via*, the island's spine, the Toledo of the island):** 8–12 m, paved, with shops behind arched ground floors.
- **Lanes (*vicoli*):** 2–4 m.
- **Steps (*gradoni*):** 2–3 m wide, about 0.15 m risers, landings every 10–12 steps.
- **Squares:** a *largo* every ~120 m, and one market lane covered with tarps.

## Plants
- Lemon trees in tubs on terraces (2–3 m).
- Bougainvillea spilling from terraces (`#c2185b`).
- Oleander in pots (`#e48aa8`).
- Basil and geraniums in window boxes (`#d1323b`).
- Fig trees in courts (5 m, broad canopy).
- Umbrella (stone) pines along the main street or plaza (12–15 m, flat crown).
- Prickly pear on rubble.

## Cyberpunk overlay
- Rooftop *superfetazioni* stacked into tin and polycarbonate towers.
- A cable web: hundreds of wires and coax spanning the lanes, sagging with the laundry.
- LED votive shrines in neon halos (blue Madonna `#4fa3ff` and red heart `#ff2b4e`).
- Holographic saints and football-icon murals as video walls.
- Scooter (drone) docks on the balconies.
- Dish clusters.
- **Night:** dim warm canyon floors (sodium `#ffb35c` from shrines and bassi doorways), with a strip of sky glowing neon above. Each shrine is its own hot spot.

  - Roof: `water_tank`, `rooftop_shack`, `antenna`, `wire_bundle`, `awning`, `market_stall`, `terrace_parapet`, `rooftop_stair_head`
  - Lights: `lantern_wall`, `luminarie_arch`
- **Unique pieces:**
  - `basso_door`: a full-width glazed street door with an inner curtain.
  - `edicola_majolica`: a shrine with tiles, a canopy and a lamp.
  - `iron_balcony_shallow`: 0.5 m deep, a pot rail and laundry hooks.
  - `slate_hip_roof` (Genoa variant).
  - `lane_buttress_beam`: a timber or stone strut spanning the lane at 3–4 m.
- **Parameters that make shared pieces read as this place:**
  - Proportions: tall and narrow, window height 2.3–2.6 m at a 1:2.4 aspect, window to wall 30%.
  - A strict vertical rhythm: windows stacked exactly in columns, a balcony on every opening.
  - A deep cornice (0.6 m) only at the top.
  - Shallow reveals (0.2 m) on rendered faces, against 0.6 m at the portone.
  - Colours: saturated earth tones per building, never white.
  - Heavy weathering.
