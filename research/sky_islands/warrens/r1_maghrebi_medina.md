# maghrebi_medina: the Maghrebi medina (Fez el-Bali, Marrakesh, Tunis)

## Sources and examples
- **Fez:**
  - Courtyard homes of 2–4 storeys with few outward openings.
  - Derbs: residential, usually dead-end, gated with a guardian (a restorer's account).
  - Streets as narrow as 0.6 m and rarely as wide as 5 m.
  - About 9,000 streets in the medina.
  - https://maghrebi-voices.swarthmore.edu/?p=427 · https://www.carfree.com/fes ·
    https://www.ragpickinghistory.co.uk/post/the-city-as-labyrinth-the-medina-of-fez ·
    https://cromwell-intl.com/travel/morocco/fez/to-my-guesthouse.html
- **Marrakesh:** dead-end alleys ending in dark small archways (sabat); the most spacious houses are on the dead
  ends. https://somorocco.com/marrakesh-medina-explained/
- **Riad:**
  - Blank thick street walls.
  - A courtyard with four planted squares round a fountain (orange and lemon); galleries of balconies on 1–3 sides.
  - Roof terraces; lime plaster, tadelakt (red and ochre) and zellige.
  - https://ryo.co/en/articles/morocco/marrakech/exceptional-riads-marrakech/ ·
    https://marrakech-riads.com/en/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/8418/
- **Fez palette:**
  - Blue, terracotta and green repeat across the city.
  - Green-tiled roofs (Qarawiyyin).
  - The Chouara tannery vats dye with indigo and red.
  - Bou Inania's green-tiled minaret.
  - https://inhand.substack.com/p/history-design-and-craft-in-fes-morocco ·
    https://ai.stanford.edu/~latombe/mountain/photo/morocco-2017/fez.htm
- **Tunis:** whitewash and blue windows; an introverted courtyard plan.
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/urban-heritage-atlas/tunis
- **Street classes and the fina':** studies of Tunis, Marrakesh, Fez and Damascus.
  https://ntnu.no/trykk/publikasjoner/hosiar_nooraddin/HTML/files/assets/basic-html/page-191.html

## Generator grammar
- **Cell and storeys:**
  - Grid 3.0 m.
  - Storeys 3.5 m at ground, 3.0 m above.
  - Houses (dar/riad) 2–4 storeys.
  - Courtyards 5–12 m in a dar; 12–25 m in a riad garden.
- **Walls:**
  - Brick and rubble, 0.5–0.7 m thick, lime-rendered.
  - Colours:
    - **Fez:** buff and ochre `#d6c19c`, `#c9a978`.
    - **Marrakesh:** the rose-red rule `#c46a4f`, `#b8573d`.
    - **Tunis:** whitewash `#f3f1ea`, with blue doors and shutters `#2d6fb0`.
  - Zellige dados (1.2–1.5 m) in courtyards and on fountains; carved plaster (gebs) and cedar above.
  - Weathering: blackened lower walls where people brush past, peeling layers of colour.
- **Mass:**
  - Fully introverted houses packed wall to wall; the medina is one continuous mass carved by lanes.
  - Bigger houses and riads at the ends of derbs.
  - Funduqs (2–3 storey inns round a court) along the main streets.
- **Streets (the most labyrinthine of the families):**
  - Tala'a and souq spines: 2–5 m, sloping, stepped in Fez; cane- or timber-covered (Marrakesh souks: slatted
    shade throwing stripes).
  - Derbs: 1–2.5 m, bending every 10–25 m, branching into dead ends.
  - **Sabats** tunnel under houses for 3–15 m.
  - Fez also has buttress arches and timber props spanning between facing walls (shoring beams), very
    characteristic.
- **Growth:**
  - E1: Idrisid/Marinid core round a mosque-university.
  - E2: houses subdivided into many dwellings; sabats added; upper floors built over the derbs.
  - E3: satellite dishes covering every roof, iron grilles, shop-front shutters.
  - E4: neon and screens in the souq canopy; roof terraces linked by catwalks.
- **Openings:**
  - Street: almost nothing. A **monumental door** (horseshoe or pointed arch, 1.4 × 2.6 m, studded, with a small
    wicket door), a few small barred windows high up.
  - Courtyard: big horseshoe arches, galleries with carved cedar balustrades, tall double doors.
  - Windows to the lane may carry small **wooden mashrabiya-like grilles** (mushrabiya-lite) and wrought-iron
    grilles.
- **Roofs:**
  - flat terraces (laundry, satellite dishes);
  - **green glazed tile** pitched roofs only on mosques, madrasas, zawiyas and gates: low hip roofs with green
    pantiles;
  - square minarets (Almohad type, height ≈ 5 × width), with a small lantern and dome on top and green tile bands;
  - **no domes on houses**.
- **Ornament:** zellige star mosaics, carved stucco, carved cedar; gates with Kufic bands. All inside, or on
  monuments.
- **Public spaces:**
  - mosque courtyards;
  - souq crossings;
  - Fez's Place Seffarine (a small triangle of coppersmiths with a big tree);
  - Marrakesh's Jemaa el-Fna (a vast open square, food stalls at night: the model for the game's plaza);
  - gate squares;
  - riad gardens (semi-public in guesthouses).
- **Water:**
  - **wall fountains** in zellige (one per quarter, 1.5 × 2 m, with a basin);
  - courtyard fountains;
  - Fez's Oued Fes canals running under the city with water mills;
  - tannery vats.
- **Plants:**
  - **bitter orange** in riads (in fours round the fountain), lemon, banana, date palm in large riads;
  - **bougainvillea** (magenta) and **jasmine** over walls;
  - **grapevine pergolas** across lanes;
  - fig trees in squares;
  - roof pots: mint and geraniums;
  - Marrakesh: palms and olive groves outside.
- **Cyberpunk overlay:**
  - Souq cane canopies become LED slat canopies throwing light stripes.
  - Zellige fountains glow from within; each derb gate has a neon number.
  - Satellite dish fields on the roofs become a rooftop "antenna sea".
  - Lantern stalls (Moroccan pierced brass lanterns) reworked with LEDs.
  - Shop-front lightboxes; cable spans on the timber shoring beams.
- **Night:** the dark labyrinth is lit by pierced-metal lanterns throwing star patterns and by the bright souqs; the
  square is a blaze of food-stall smoke and bulbs.

## Kit
- **Common pieces:**
  - `wall_panel` (thick rendered, colour per city);
  - `door_studded` (horseshoe portal), `arch_opening` (horseshoe, lobed);
  - `tile_panel` (zellige dado), `gallery_timber` (courtyard), `window_small` + `reja_grille`;
  - `sabat_bridge`, `covered_alley`, a shoring beam (`plank_bridge` preset);
  - `green_tile_eave` / `roof_hip_tile` (green) for monuments;
  - `minaret_small` (square Almohad preset);
  - `fountain_basin` (zellige wall fountain), `market_stall`, `awning` (cane slat), `lantern` (pierced brass);
  - citrus, bougainvillea, vines.
- **Parameters that make it Maghrebi:**
  - the street facade is 90% blank wall;
  - one rich door every 8–15 m;
  - lanes bend every 10–25 m with many dead ends and sabats;
  - one colour per city (ochre, pink-red, white with blue);
  - green tile only on sacred buildings;
  - square minarets;
  - courtyards with orange trees in fours.
- **Unique pieces (3):**
  - `zellige_fountain` (wall fountain);
  - `minaret_square` (Almohad, green tile bands);
  - `shoring_beam` (a timber prop between facing walls).
