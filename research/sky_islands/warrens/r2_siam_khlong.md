# Bangkok: khlong canal life and Yaowarat (Thonburi, Chinatown)

**Mark:** Teak houses on stilts with steep gabled roofs and curling finials lining canals of floating markets, and behind them a Chinatown of gold-shop neon over two-storey shophouses.

Sources: Thonburi khlongs; Yaowarat Chinatown; Talat Noi; soi networks

## How it grew
- Ayutthaya-Rattanakosin: settlements follow waterways; houses on stilts face the canals; temples (wat) on the banks; floating markets.
- Rama IV-VI: Sino-colonial shophouses along new roads (Yaowarat), 2-3 storeys.
- Later: concrete row-houses on sois, canals filled east of the river, kept in Thonburi.

## Generator grammar
- **Cell:** 4.0 x 4.0 m; storey 3.2 m; 1-5 storeys.
- **Walls:** teak plank panels (stilt houses); stucco and concrete (shophouses); 0.08 m thick; palette #6b4a2e, #8a5f3a, #e8d8b8, #c8a040, #2f6a8a, #a8322a; weathering: silver-grey weathered teak, green water stains.
- **Mass:** Canal fronts of stilt houses 1-2 storeys on piles over the water, piers and steps; behind, rows of shophouses on sois; temples with tiered roofs.
- **Streets:**
  - main khlong: 15 m (boats, floating market)
  - road (Yaowarat): 14 m (neon, gold shops)
  - soi: 5 m (market stalls)
  - canal walkway: 1.5 m (raised concrete/timber path along the houses)
- **Growth, in layers:**
  - stilt: teak houses on piles, piers, temples [teak, piles, tile]
  - shophouse: Sino-colonial rows [stucco, brick]
  - overgrowth: tin extensions over water, rooftop sheds, wires [zinc, concrete]
  - cyberpunk: gold-shop neon, LED boats, screens [LED]
- **Openings:** Wide timber panel windows hinged to swing out; shophouse shutters.
- **Roofs:** Steep (45 deg+) gable roofs with curling chofa finials on houses and tiered layered roofs with nagas on temples; shophouses flat or low tile.
- **Ornament:** Temple gold, mirror mosaics, naga finials; spirit houses on posts.
- **Public spaces:** Temple courtyards, floating market basins, a riverside park; on an island: a canal ring with a floating market basin, a temple plaza, a park with a pond.
- **Water:** Canals threading the island, a market basin.
- **Street hierarchy:** khlong 15 m / road 14 m > sois 5 m > canal walks 1.5 m

## Foliage
- banana, coconut palm: clumps and palms, 3-15 m, canal banks (#5f9a3e)
- bodhi tree (Ficus religiosa): large, heart leaves, wrapped in coloured cloth, 10-20 m, temples (#3f6a35)
- lotus, water hyacinth: floating, 0-1 m, canals, ponds (#5f9a3e)
- potted bougainvillea, orchids: pots on piers, 0.5-2 m, piers (#d8307a)

## Cyberpunk overlay
- gold-shop neon (red and gold characters)
- LED boats
- spirit houses lit
- screens on temples' edges
- **Night:** Red and gold neon canyon of Yaowarat, canals of lanterns and boat lights, temples floodlit gold.
