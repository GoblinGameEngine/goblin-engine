# Tibetan Lhasa: the Barkhor and its whitewashed courtyard houses

**Mark:** Battered whitewashed stone walls leaning inward, windows framed in black trapezoids with little cloth valances, flat roofs with prayer flags on every corner, a kora circling a temple.

Sources: Lhasa old city, the Barkhor; Potala; Gyantse; Ladakh (Leh)

## How it grew
- 7th c.-: the Jokhang and the Barkhor kora ring round it; noble houses of 3-4 storeys round courtyards.
- Walls of stone (granite) and mud brick, battered: thick at the base (>1 m), thinner above, the inner face vertical; flat roofs of earth (arga) on timber beams.
- Later: concrete houses in the same idiom; shop fronts in the ground floor; extra storeys.

## Generator grammar
- **Cell:** 3.0 x 3.0 m; storey 2.9 m; 2-5 storeys.
- **Walls:** whitewashed stone and mud brick, battered (5-8 degrees); maroon-red (pembe-style twig frieze) band under the parapet on important buildings; 1.0 m thick; palette #f1ede4, #e5ddcd, #7a2a24, #2a2a2a, #c9a33a, #3d5f8f; weathering: whitewash poured from the roof leaves streaks; dust.
- **Mass:** Courtyard blocks (15-30 m) of 3-4 storeys facing a ring street round the temple; stepped back on the slope; a few great masses (a Potala-like palace up the hill).
- **Streets:**
  - kora (ring street): 8 m (clockwise pilgrim circuit round the temple; stalls)
  - radial street: 5 m (from the kora outwards)
  - lane: 2.5 m (to courtyards)
  - courtyard gallery: 1.5 m (timber balconies round the court)
- **Growth, in layers:**
  - old: whitewashed courtyard houses, temples, chortens [stone, mud brick, timber, arga]
  - later: extra storeys, shopfronts, glazed sunrooms on roofs [concrete, glass]
  - cyberpunk: prayer flags of LED, mani walls of screens, lit window frames [LED]
- **Openings:** Trapezoidal black-painted window surrounds wider at the bottom; carved and painted timber lintels in tiers; short pleated cloth valances (shambu) above windows and doors.
- **Roofs:** Flat, earth-packed, parapets; prayer-flag poles at the corners; gilded roof ornaments on temples.
- **Ornament:** Painted lintels (red, blue, green, gold), brass, the maroon frieze band.
- **Public spaces:** The temple square and the kora; incense burners; a park with willows (lingka); on an island: the kora as the large paved ring street, a temple square, a willow park with a pond.
- **Water:** A pond in a willow park; water carried in; snow.
- **Street hierarchy:** kora ring 8 m > radial streets 5 m > lanes 2.5 m > courtyard galleries

## Foliage
- willow (Salix): pollarded rounded crowns, 5-10 m, parks, lingka (#7a9a4a)
- poplar (white poplar, ja-kar): tall columnar, 10-18 m, lanes, parks (#9ab25a)
- potted marigolds and geraniums: pots on windowsills and roofs, 0.2-0.5 m, window ledges, roof parapets (#e88a2a / #c4304a)

## Cyberpunk overlay
- LED prayer-flag strings in the five colours
- screens behind the black window frames
- lit gold roof ornaments
- projected mantras on whitewash
- **Night:** White walls glowing pale under the moon, gold temple roofs lit, strings of five-colour flags luminous, butter-lamp warmth inside windows.
