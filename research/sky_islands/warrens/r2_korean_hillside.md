# Korean hillside villages: daldongne, Gamcheon and the rooftop room

**Mark:** Terraced cubes of concrete stacked up a steep slope so every roof is a view, pastel boxes with blue and green flat roofs and water tanks, a rooftop room (oktapbang) on many, and long stairs with no streets.

Sources: Gamcheon Culture Village, Busan; Seoul daldongne (Ihwa, Baeksa); oktapbang rooftop rooms

## How it grew
- 1950s: Korean War refugees build shacks of scrap wood, rocks and corrugated iron on steep hills.
- 1970s-80s: rebuilt as 1-3 storey concrete-block and brick houses on the same terraces.
- 2009-: artists paint them pastel; murals, sculptures; rooftop rooms and green-painted waterproofed roofs.

## Generator grammar
- **Cell:** 5.0 x 5.0 m; storey 2.8 m; 1-4 storeys.
- **Walls:** concrete block and brick, rendered and painted; 0.2 m thick; palette #f2c8a8, #a8d8e8, #f8e8a0, #c8e8b8, #e8b8d0, #6a9ad0, #4a9a6a; weathering: peeling pastel, rust from railings.
- **Mass:** Rows of small cubic houses on narrow terraces following contours, each roof below the next row's street level; no gaps along a row; stair streets straight up the slope.
- **Streets:**
  - ridge road: 7 m (the one bus road along the top)
  - contour lane: 2.5 m (along each terrace)
  - stair: 1.5 m (straight up the slope, 50-150 steps, railings)
- **Growth, in layers:**
  - shacks: scrap huts [timber, corrugated, stone]
  - houses: 1-3 storey concrete cubes [concrete block, brick]
  - rooftop: oktapbang rooms, tanks, satellite dishes, chili drying, pot gardens [panel, plastic]
  - cyberpunk: LED murals, neon crosses (churches), lit stairs [LED]
- **Openings:** Aluminium sliding windows with security bars, small; metal doors.
- **Roofs:** Flat, painted green/blue waterproofing; blue/stainless water tanks; jangdok soy jars; laundry.
- **Ornament:** Murals, painted stairs, fish sculptures.
- **Public spaces:** A viewpoint plaza at the top, a small market, a church, a pocket park; on an island: a summit plaza and garden, a terraced park with a pond at the bottom.
- **Water:** Tanks; a pond at the foot.
- **Street hierarchy:** ridge road 7 m > contour lanes 2.5 m > stairs 1.5 m
