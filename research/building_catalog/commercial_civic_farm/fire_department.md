# Volunteer Fire Department

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All tiers — rural village has a small 1-2 bay volunteer station; small town has 2-4 bays; small city has a larger multi-bay station, possibly combined with EMS, and may have more than one station.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: Rural village: 8-12m x 10-15m. Small town: 12-18m x 15-22m. Small city: 18-28m x 20-35m.
- **Typical story count**: 1 story with tall apparatus bay ceilings (4.5-6m), occasionally a 2nd-floor meeting hall/bunk room above the office/day-room portion.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Utilitarian brick or metal-sided building dominated by 1-4 large overhead garage doors facing the street, a hose-drying tower (tall narrow element) in older examples, siren/alert horn mounted high on the facade.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Apparatus bay**: 4-5m x 8-12m per bay, 1-4 bays
- **Day room/meeting room**: 5-7m x 6-9m
- **Office (fire chief)**: 2.5m x 3m
- **Gear/turnout storage**: 3-4m x 4-6m, bay-adjacent
- **Kitchen/break area**: 3m x 4m
- **Restroom**: 1.5m x 2m
- **Hose tower (older/rural examples)**: 2m x 2m footprint, full building height
- **Bunk room (small city, if staffed)**: 4m x 6m

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Apparatus bay**: fire truck(s)/pumper (1 per bay), hose reels (wall-mounted, 1-2 per bay), tool boards, workbench (0-1).
- **Day room**: table + chairs (1 set, 6-10), TV/radio scanner (1), couch (0-1), bulletin board (call schedule/training notices).
- **Gear storage**: turnout coat/boot racks (1 rack per volunteer roster, 10-30 slots), helmet shelving.
- **Kitchen**: coffee maker, fridge, small stove, cabinets.
- **Office**: desk, filing cabinet, radio base station.

## Placement Notes
- Apparatus bays always face the street directly (fastest possible exit for trucks) with wide overhead doors as the dominant facade feature.
- Gear storage is immediately adjacent to the apparatus bay for fast turnout (never on a separate floor from the trucks).
- Day room/office are set back from the bays, often along a side or rear wing.
- Rural-village tier: single bay + a small unheated gear alcove, day room doubles as the only indoor gathering space (no separate office).

## Sources
- General volunteer-fire-station typology consistent with small-municipal-building conventions referenced elsewhere in this catalog (apparatus-bay-dominant facade is a well-documented universal convention for this building type).
