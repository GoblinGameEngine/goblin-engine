# Restaurant (Sit-Down, Non-Diner)

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Small towns (one or two "nicer" sit-down restaurants alongside diners/cafes) and small cities (a broader range, including ethnic/specialty restaurants). Rural villages typically lack a distinct sit-down restaurant beyond the cafe/tavern.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 8-14m wide x 15-25m deep (25-45ft x 50-80ft).
- **Typical story count**: 1 story, occasionally 2 (banquet room or apartment upstairs).
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Storefront or free-standing depending on era; storefront examples resemble other Main Street retail with more subdued/formal window treatment (curtains, lower sill height); free-standing suburban-edge examples (small-city tier) have their own parking lot and a more distinct roof form.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Dining room(s)**: 7-11m x 10-16m, sometimes split into 2 rooms (main + banquet/private)
- **Bar/waiting area (optional)**: 3-4m x 4-6m near entrance
- **Kitchen**: 5-7m x 6-9m, rear
- **Walk-in cooler**: 2-2.5m x 3m, kitchen-adjacent
- **Restrooms (2)**: 1.5m x 2m each
- **Office**: 2m x 2.5m
- **Banquet/private dining room (small city tier, optional)**: 5-7m x 6-9m

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Dining room**: tables/chairs, mixed 2-6 top (8-18 sets), booths along walls (0-6), host stand (1), sideboard/server station (1-2), decor (art, plants).
- **Bar/waiting area**: small bar counter (0-1), stools (0-6), bench seating.
- **Kitchen**: range/oven (1-2), grill (1), fryer (0-1), prep tables (2-3), dish pit/sink station (1), walk-in access, shelving.
- **Restrooms**: standard fixtures.
- **Office**: desk, safe, small filing cabinet.

## Placement Notes
- Kitchen is always rear/alley-adjacent for deliveries, matching the service-lane system.
- Host stand sits immediately inside the entrance, controlling sightlines into the dining room.
- Small-city free-standing variant gets an attached small parking lot in front rather than relying purely on street parking — flag this archetype as "parking-lot compatible" for the site-placement pass.
