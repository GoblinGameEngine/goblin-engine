# Small Factory / Mill

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Small town (a single-product mill or light manufacturer — feed mill, creamery/dairy processing plant, small furniture or implement factory) and small city (larger manufacturer, possibly multiple buildings). Rural villages typically lack this archetype, though a small feed/flour mill may appear as a scaled-down variant near the grain elevator.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: Small town mill: 15-25m x 25-40m. Small city factory: 25-45m x 40-80m, sometimes multiple connected wings.
- **Typical story count**: 1-3 stories depending on process type (flour/feed mills tend to be vertical, gravity-fed; furniture/metal shops tend to be single-story with a sawtooth or monitor roof for daylight).
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Brick or heavy-timber mill construction (older examples) or corrugated-metal pre-engineered building (newer examples), tall multi-pane factory windows or monitor/sawtooth roof for daylighting the shop floor, prominent smokestack or grain-mill headhouse as a landmark silhouette, rail spur access typical.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Main production floor**: 12-25m x 20-40m, open volume with tall ceiling (5-8m)
- **Raw material receiving/storage**: 6-10m x 10-15m, dock-adjacent
- **Finished goods storage**: 6-10m x 10-15m
- **Office/front administration**: 5m x 8m, street-facing
- **Break room/locker room**: 4m x 6m
- **Boiler/mechanical room**: 4m x 5m
- **Loading dock**: 3-4m x 4m per bay, 2-4 bays

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Production floor**: process-specific machinery rows (milling stones/rollers, sawmill/woodworking equipment, or metal-fabrication stations depending on the mill's product — pick one theme per instance), conveyor lines (0-2), overhead line-shaft or crane track (older examples), workstations (4-12).
- **Receiving/storage**: pallet racks, raw material bins/hoppers.
- **Finished goods storage**: pallet racks, crated product stacks, shipping desk.
- **Office**: desks (2-5), drafting table (0-1), filing cabinets, sample product display case.
- **Break room**: tables, lockers, vending (small city tier).
- **Boiler room**: boiler unit, coal or fuel-oil storage, gauges/piping.

## Placement Notes
- Production floor's machinery theme should be chosen once per instance and applied consistently to prop selection (e.g., a "feed mill" instance gets grinding/mixing equipment and grain hoppers; a "furniture shop" instance gets woodworking machinery and lumber racks) rather than mixing themes within one building.
- Loading docks and raw-material storage are on the rail-spur/alley side; the office/administration is on the public-street side — mirrors the warehouse and grain elevator siting logic.
- Best sited in the light-industrial fringe zone, typically clustered with the warehouse and grain elevator.
