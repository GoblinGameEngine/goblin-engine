# Barn (Gambrel-Roof Dairy/General Barn)

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Farm zone, all settlement tiers (farms surround/feed rural villages and small towns especially, but farmsteads appear in the farm zone regardless of the nearest settlement's size).
- **Typical exterior footprint**: Main gambrel-roofed core: 11-13m wide x 12-14m deep (documented historic examples: 38'5"x42' and 38'x44'). Lean-to sheds commonly added to one or two sides for stalls/milking parlor add another 4-6m depth per side.
- **Typical story count**: 2 levels — ground-floor stalls/equipment bay, upper hay mow — plus lean-to shed additions at ground level only.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Wood-frame with vertical board or lapped-board siding, painted red (traditional) or left to weather, the distinctive two-slope gambrel roofline (steep lower slope, shallower upper slope) to maximize hay-mow volume, hay hood/track projecting from the gable peak for hoisting loose hay, cupola(s) on the roof ridge for ventilation, large sliding or hinged barn doors on the gable end(s).

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Main bay/threshing floor (ground level, center aisle)**: 4-6m x 10-14m
- **Stalls (dairy cows/horses, ground level, flanking the center aisle or in a lean-to shed)**: 1.1-1.5m x 2.5m per stall, 8-24 stalls depending on farm size
- **Tack room/feed room**: 3m x 4m, ground level
- **Milking parlor (dairy-specific lean-to addition)**: 4m x 6m
- **Hay mow (upper level, full footprint of the gambrel-roofed core)**: 11m x 12-14m, tall peaked volume
- **Loft ladder/stairs**: 1m x 2m footprint, connects ground floor to mow

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Main bay**: hay bales/loose hay stacks (variable, fills much of the mow rather than the main bay), farm equipment (tractor, wagon, plow/disc/planter implements — 2-5 pieces), workbench (1), tool wall (hand tools, harness, 1 wall's worth).
- **Stalls**: stanchions or stall partitions (1 per stall), feed troughs (1 per stall or shared trough run), water bowls/buckets.
- **Tack room**: harness racks (1), saddle stands (0-2, if horses present), feed bins (2-4), grain scoop, medicine cabinet.
- **Milking parlor**: milking stanchions (4-8), milk cans or bulk tank (1), wash sink.
- **Hay mow**: loose hay or baled hay stacks (fills 40-90% of volume depending on season), hay track/trolley mechanism along the ridge, pitchfork(s), occasional barn-cat nest/pigeon roost as set dressing.

## Placement Notes
- Main bay doors are always centered on a gable end, wide enough for a wagon/tractor to drive straight through (through-barn convention) when the barn design supports it.
- Hay mow is loaded from the gable-end hay hood/door, so keep that opening aligned with the exterior hay-hoist prop.
- Stalls/milking parlor are placed in ground-level lean-to sheds along the barn's long sides, keeping the center aisle clear for equipment movement.
- Site adjacent to the farmhouse and within the farmstead cluster (near the silo, corn crib, and machine shed) rather than isolated.
