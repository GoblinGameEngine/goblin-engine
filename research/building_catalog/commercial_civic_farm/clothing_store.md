# Clothing Store

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Small towns (one general clothing/dry-goods store) and small cities (multiple specialized stores: men's, women's, children's, shoe store variant). Rural villages typically fold this into the general store.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 7-12m wide x 15-25m deep (25-40ft x 50-80ft).
- **Typical story count**: 1-2 stories; second floor used for alterations/stock or a fitting mezzanine in larger examples.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Brick storefront with tall display windows for mannequins, recessed entry, often decorative cornice. Interior fixtures more finished (wood/glass cases) than utilitarian stores.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Sales floor**: 6-10m x 10-18m
- **Fitting rooms**: 1.2m x 1.5m each, clustered (2-4 rooms)
- **Stockroom**: 4-6m x 6-9m
- **Alterations/sewing room**: 3m x 3.5m (small city tier)
- **Office**: 2.5m x 3m
- **Restroom**: 1.5m x 2m

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Sales floor**:
  - Clothing racks (freestanding + wall) (8-16)
  - Shelving (folded goods, shoes) (4-8 units)
  - Mannequins (2-5)
  - Checkout counter + register (1)
  - Full-length mirrors (2-4)
  - Display tables (2-4)
- **Fitting rooms**: bench/stool, mirror, hook row, curtain or door per room.
- **Stockroom**: garment racks (bulk) (4-6), shelving (boxed shoes/stock) (3-5), steamer (1).
- **Alterations room**: sewing machine (1-2), cutting table (1), pin cushion/notions shelf.
- **Office**: desk, safe, ledger.

## Placement Notes
- Sales floor fronts the street with display-window mannequins; fitting rooms are set back along a side wall, never street-facing (privacy).
- Stockroom is rear/alley-adjacent for shipment deliveries.
- In small-city tier, generate 1-3 of these as specialized variants (menswear, women's wear, shoes) by swapping the sales-floor fixture pool bias rather than the room list.
