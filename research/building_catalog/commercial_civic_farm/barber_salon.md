# Barber Shop / Salon

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All tiers — smallest villages support one barber shop; small towns/cities support several, and may add dedicated beauty salons as a separate archetype variant.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 4-7m wide x 8-14m deep (13-23ft x 25-45ft) — one of the smallest storefront footprints.
- **Typical story count**: 1 story (ground floor of a larger mixed-use block is common).
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Narrow storefront, single door + display window, barber pole mounted at entrance. Often occupies the narrowest leftover lot slice between larger commercial buildings.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Main floor (chairs + waiting)**: 4-6m x 6-10m
- **Small back room (storage/break)**: 2m x 2.5m
- **Restroom**: 1.2m x 1.8m

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Main floor**:
  - Barber/styling chairs (2-5)
  - Wall mirrors (1 per chair)
  - Styling station counters/cabinets (1 per chair)
  - Waiting bench/chairs (1 set, 3-5 seats)
  - Magazine/newspaper rack (1)
  - Barber pole (exterior-mounted, 1)
  - Sink/shampoo station (1-2, salon variant)
  - Hot towel warmer / straight-razor strop (barber variant, 0-1)
- **Back room**: shelving (supplies, towels), small washer (salon variant), coat hooks.
- **Restroom**: toilet, sink.

## Placement Notes
- Always single street-facing storefront with 1-2 chairs visible through the window — an intentionally social/visible business.
- No alley/rear-delivery requirement — smallest footprint archetype, can fill narrow infill lots between larger buildings.
- Barber pole placement is a mandatory exterior prop for generator identification/readability from the street.
