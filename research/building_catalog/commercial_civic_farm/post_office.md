# Post Office

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All tiers — rural villages have a small standalone or general-store-integrated post office; small towns/cities have a dedicated purpose-built post office, often a standardized federal design.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: Rural village: 5-8m x 8-12m (tiny standalone). Small town: 10-15m x 15-22m. Small city: 15-22m x 25-35m, sometimes with a separate loading area.
- **Typical story count**: 1 story (occasionally 2 in small-city main post offices, with upstairs offices).
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Brick or stone, restrained federal/classical civic styling (small pediment, flagpole, symmetrical facade), distinct from commercial storefronts — no large plate-glass display windows, instead a row of smaller windows.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Public lobby (PO boxes + service counter)**: 5-8m x 8-14m
- **Service counter/window area**: 1.5m deep counter run, 3-6m long
- **Sorting room (workroom floor)**: 6-10m x 8-15m, rear
- **Postmaster's office**: 2.5m x 3m
- **Loading dock/mail truck bay**: 3-4m x 4-5m, rear
- **Restroom (staff)**: 1.5m x 2m

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Lobby**: wall of PO boxes (brass-doored, 50-500 depending on tier), service counter with scale and register (1), stamp/form displays, standing writing counter (1), bulletin board (wanted posters/notices) (1).
- **Sorting room**: sorting case/pigeonhole shelving (2-4 units), work tables (2-3), mail bins/hampers (several), canvas mail sacks, scale (1).
- **Postmaster's office**: desk, filing cabinet, safe.
- **Loading dock**: hand truck, mail bags, dock door.

## Placement Notes
- PO box wall is always along an interior wall of the public lobby, visible/accessible without staff assistance; service counter separates public lobby from the staff-only sorting room.
- Sorting room and loading dock are rear/alley-adjacent for mail-truck access — align with the alley/service-lane system.
- Rural-village tier: collapse to a single room (PO boxes + tiny counter, no separate sorting room) — often physically inside or attached to the general store.

## Sources
- General USPS standard small-town post office typology; cross-referenced with NRHP Main Street civic-building nomination conventions (symmetrical facade, public lobby + workroom split) used across the sources above.
