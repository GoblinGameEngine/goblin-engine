# Town Hall / Municipal Building

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All tiers — rural village town hall may be a single multi-purpose room; small town/city versions have distinct departments (clerk, utilities billing, council chambers).
- **Typical exterior footprint**: Rural village: 8-12m x 10-15m. Small town: 12-18m x 18-25m. Small city: 18-25m x 25-40m (may combine with city offices).
- **Typical story count**: 1-2 stories.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Brick or stone civic architecture, often with a small clock tower, cupola, or flagpole as a landmark feature; symmetrical facade, steps up to a formal entrance.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Lobby/reception**: 4-6m x 6-8m
- **Council chambers/meeting hall**: 8-12m x 10-16m (doubles as community meeting space in small tier)
- **Clerk's office/records room**: 3m x 4m
- **Utility billing/treasurer's office**: 3m x 4m (small town/city)
- **Mayor's/administrator's office**: 3m x 3.5m
- **Restrooms (2)**: 1.5m x 2m each
- **Basement/storage (records archive)**: matches footprint, half

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Lobby**: reception desk (1), bulletin board/notices (1), bench seating (0-2), flag (1-2).
- **Council chambers**: dais/raised table for council (1), podium (1), rows of public seating (20-80 seats depending on tier), American + state flags (2).
- **Clerk's office**: desk, filing cabinets (3-6), record books/safe.
- **Utility billing office**: counter window, desk, filing cabinets, cash drawer.
- **Mayor's office**: desk, bookshelf, small meeting table.
- **Storage/archive**: shelving, boxed records, old ledgers.

## Placement Notes
- Council chambers is the largest room and typically occupies the rear or a dedicated wing, with public entrance access separate from staff-office corridor when the building is large enough (small city tier).
- Lobby is always the street-facing entry space; offices branch off a central corridor behind it.
- Rural-village tier: single multipurpose room serves as both council chambers and lobby; clerk's desk sits in a corner of that same room.
