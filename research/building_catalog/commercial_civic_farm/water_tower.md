# Water Tower (Structure Type, No Interior)

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All tiers — a near-universal Midwestern small-town landmark, usually painted with the town name. Rural villages may instead use a simpler ground-level standpipe or a smaller elevated tank; small cities may have 2+ towers.
- **Typical exterior footprint (base)**: 8-15m diameter support structure footprint; tank itself 6-12m diameter.
- **Typical total height**: 25-45m to the top of the tank (rural village/small town: 25-35m; small city: 35-45m for a multi-legged standpipe or fluted-column tank).
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Steel riveted or welded tank on a 4-6 leg steel truss tower (classic "golf ball on tees" silhouette), or a single fluted steel column ("hydro-pillar" style) in newer/larger examples. Painted with town name/logo. Small ladder/catwalk visible but not a walkable interior for generation purposes.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **No enclosed interior spaces.** This archetype is a non-interior landmark/skyline structure only.
- Optional: small fenced equipment/valve house at the base, 2m x 2m, if a ground-level pump/valve room is desired — treat as a trivial single-room utility shed, not a furnished space.

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Base valve house (optional)**: valve wheel/control panel, small workbench, no other furnishing needed.

## Placement Notes
- Site at the highest local elevation point available in the settlement, or centrally for visibility — functions as a skyline landmark and wayfinding reference visible from most of the settlement.
- No gameplay interior; treat purely as an exterior prop/structure for skyline composition and as a possible climbable exterior ladder feature if the game supports that kind of traversal.
- One per settlement is typical for village/town tier; small city may generate 2.

## Sources
- General Midwestern municipal water-tower typology (steel elevated tank on truss legs) — well-documented common knowledge, no single citation needed beyond general civil-infrastructure convention.
