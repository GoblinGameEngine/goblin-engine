# Hardware Store

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All three tiers. Present even in small villages (often combined with a farm-implement dealership); a dedicated hardware store with wider stock is typical of small towns and small cities.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 9-15m wide x 18-30m deep (30-50ft x 60-100ft) — one of the deeper Main Street lots because of stockroom needs.
- **Typical story count**: 1-2 stories; second floor (when present) is stock overflow or a separate rentable office/apartment.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Brick commercial block, large street-level display windows, often a recessed entry with a secondary service/lumber door on the alley side. Exterior sometimes has hardware displayed outside (wheelbarrows, ladders) under an awning.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Sales floor**: 8-15m x 10-20m
- **Back stockroom**: 6-10m x 8-14m (larger than most retail — hardware needs deep inventory)
- **Office**: 2.5-4m x 3-4m
- **Restroom**: 1.5m x 2m
- **Paint-mixing/key-cutting nook**: 2m x 2.5m (small towns/cities only)
- **Loading dock/alley bay**: 3-4m x 4-5m, rear

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Sales floor**:
  - Shelving units, floor-to-ceiling (10-20)
  - Checkout counter + register (1)
  - Nail/screw/fastener bin wall, small drawers (1 unit)
  - Barrels (nails, rope) (2-5)
  - Tool wall (hand tools hung on pegboard) (1 large display)
  - Ladder display / rolling ladder for high shelves (1-2)
  - Paint can display + mixer (1, small towns+)
  - Seasonal aisle end-caps (garden, holiday) (1-3)
  - Glass display case (knives, small tools) (1-2)
- **Stockroom**:
  - Heavy-duty pallet racks (4-8)
  - Crated bulk goods (lumber scraps, pipe, chain) (several stacks)
  - Hand truck / dolly (1-2)
  - Workbench for assembly/repairs (1)
- **Office**: desk, filing cabinet, safe, ledger books.
- **Loading bay**: overhead or barn-style double door, hand truck ramp.

## Placement Notes
- Sales floor always has the street-facing display window and main entrance.
- Stockroom is always rear/alley-adjacent for deliveries — matches the alley system already in downtown zones.
- In rural-village tier, merge with farm-implement/feed functions: add a small "implement corner" to the sales floor pool (harness, fence wire spools, plow parts).
- Loading bay door should align with the alley network for delivery-truck pathing.
