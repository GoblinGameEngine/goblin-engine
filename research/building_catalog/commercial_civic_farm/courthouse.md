# Courthouse (City Tier Only)

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Small city (30,000-50,000) only — a county courthouse is a county-seat institution, so it should be generated at most once per station/region, sited on the small city. Not applicable to rural villages or small towns unless one is explicitly designated the county seat.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 25-35m x 30-45m (historic example: interior ~95ft x 68ft / ~29m x 21m core, plus surrounding office wings). Frequently sited on its own landscaped "courthouse square" block, not a standard commercial lot.
- **Typical story count**: 2-3 stories plus raised basement, per the classic base/piano-nobile/attic massing.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Stone or masonry with classical detailing (columns, pediment, dome or cupola common), rusticated raised basement, formal entrance stairs on all four sides in the most elaborate examples (single primary entrance in simpler ones), strictly symmetrical about both axes.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Main courtroom**: 10-16m x 14-20m, tall ceiling (5-8m), often with a public balcony/gallery
- **Judge's chambers**: 4m x 5m, courtroom-adjacent
- **Jury room**: 4m x 5m
- **Clerk of courts office**: 5m x 7m
- **County offices (treasurer, assessor, recorder, etc.)**: 4-6m x 5-8m each, 3-6 offices
- **Central rotunda/lobby**: 8-12m diameter or equivalent square footprint, often domed/skylit
- **Grand staircase**: spans full building height, central or dual symmetrical stairs
- **Holding cell/detention (small, courthouse-adjacent)**: 2m x 2.5m, 1-2 cells
- **Basement records vault/archive**: matches footprint, half

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Main courtroom**: judge's bench (1, elevated), witness stand (1), jury box (12-14 seats), counsel tables (2), gallery/public seating (40-150), bailiff's desk (1), state/federal flags (2).
- **Rotunda/lobby**: directory board (1), benches (2-4), historical portraits/plaques, decorative dome or skylight feature (non-furniture, architectural).
- **County offices**: counters, desks (2-6 per office), filing cabinets (heavy — county records), microfiche/records storage (small-city era detail).
- **Jury room**: conference table, 12-14 chairs, small coat closet.
- **Records vault**: fireproof shelving, ledger/deed books, map cabinets.

## Placement Notes
- Courtroom is centered on the upper floor along the primary axis, directly above or behind the rotunda, reflecting the ground-floor-offices/upper-floor-courtroom split documented in the source material.
- Rotunda/central lobby is the organizing hub — all four office wings and the grand stair radiate from it, reinforcing the mandated bilateral symmetry.
- Building is best sited on a dedicated square/block with its own perimeter green space rather than a standard storefront lot — flag for special-lot placement in the small-city zone.
- Only generate one courthouse per station map; all other civic buildings (town hall, library, police) are per-settlement, but the courthouse is per-county-seat.
