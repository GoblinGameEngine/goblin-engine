# Auto Repair Shop

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All tiers — distinct from the gas station archetype when a settlement is large enough to support a dedicated garage without fuel pumps (small town/city); rural villages typically combine repair function into the gas station instead (use `gas_station.md` for that case).
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 12-20m wide x 15-25m deep.
- **Typical story count**: 1 story, tall bay ceiling (4-5.5m).
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Utilitarian metal-sided or concrete-block building, 2-4 large overhead bay doors dominating the front facade, small office with a walk-in customer door beside the bays, sometimes a used-car/parts lot to one side.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Service bays**: 4-5m x 8-10m each, 2-5 bays
- **Customer office/waiting area**: 3m x 4m
- **Parts storage room**: 4m x 6m
- **Tool/equipment room**: 3m x 4m
- **Restroom**: 1.5m x 2m
- **Scrap/tire yard (exterior, adjacent)**: fenced side lot

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Service bays**: hydraulic lifts (1 per bay), tool chests (1-2 per bay), air compressor (1, shared), engine hoist (0-1), workbenches, tire changer/balancer (0-1 per shop), vehicles in various repair states (1-2 per bay).
- **Office**: counter, register, waiting chairs (2-4), parts catalogs, phone.
- **Parts storage**: shelving (belts, filters, fluids, hardware bins), tire stack.
- **Tool room**: pegboard tool wall, rolling cabinets, welding equipment (0-1, small city tier).

## Placement Notes
- Service bays always front the street or a forecourt with overhead doors for vehicle access; office sits beside the bays with a window overlooking them.
- Parts/tool rooms are interior, windowless, adjacent to the bays for quick access.
- Exterior scrap/tire yard, when present, is fenced and to the side or rear — not street-facing.

## Sources
- General small-town auto-repair-garage typology, consistent with the gas-station "oblong box"/bay-fronted convention documented in the sources for `gas_station.md`.
