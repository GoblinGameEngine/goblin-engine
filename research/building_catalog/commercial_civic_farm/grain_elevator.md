# Grain Elevator

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All tiers, but essential in rural villages and small towns as a farm-economy anchor; small cities may have a larger terminal-scale elevator or several smaller ones on rail spurs. Sited at town edge, always adjacent to a rail line or, on the station, the equivalent transport corridor.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: Wood cribbed elevator: 9-15m x 12-20m footprint at the base (bin house), plus an attached driveway/scale structure. Height dominates over footprint — the headhouse rises well above the bin section.
- **Typical story count**: Functionally 4-8 "stories" of vertical bin space (not walkable floors in the usual sense) plus a headhouse at the top (1-2 working levels) and a ground-level driveway/office level.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Vertical wood board siding (cribbed wood-bin construction) or corrugated metal, tall narrow massing, small headhouse cupola at the peak, an attached lower single-story office/scale building at the base, side-loading rail spur or truck driveway running through or alongside the structure.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Driveway/truck dump**: 3-4m wide x 8-15m long, passes through or alongside the base of the structure — trucks drive in to unload
- **Scale house/office**: 3m x 4m, attached at the driveway entrance
- **Ground-level workroom (leg pit, drive machinery)**: 4m x 5m
- **Grain bins (bulk storage, non-walkable interior volume)**: stacked/clustered, 8-20 bins per elevator, each roughly 3-4m x 3-4m footprint
- **Headhouse (top working level)**: 3m x 4m, houses the distributor and leg-drive machinery
- **Stairwell (2 flights, following the leg/manlift)**: runs full height along one wall

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Scale house/office**: truck scale (1, in the driveway), ledger desk (1), grain-grading samples/test kit (1), small safe (1), wall-mounted grade/price board.
- **Driveway/dump**: grate/dump pit (1), overhead spouts, dust curtains.
- **Workroom (leg pit)**: bucket-elevator "leg" mechanism (1, vertical, full height), drive motor, belts/pulleys, tool bench.
- **Headhouse**: distributor spout mechanism, small work platform, single bare-bulb lighting.
- **Bins**: mostly unfurnished bulk-storage volume — treat as non-walkable "set dressing" volumes rather than furnished rooms; optionally expose one bin's inspection hatch/ladder as an interactable.

## Placement Notes
- Elevator must be sited adjacent to a rail spur (or the equivalent linear transport feature in the station's zoning) — this is a hard placement rule, not a preference.
- Driveway passes straight through or alongside the ground floor so a truck can enter, dump, and exit without reversing — keep it a through-lane, not a dead end.
- Office/scale house is the only human-scale furnished room; the bulk of the structure (the bins and headhouse) should be treated as mostly non-interactive vertical mass for interior generation purposes, with the leg pit and headhouse as small climbable/explorable spaces along a single stairwell.
- Best placed at the edge of downtown or in a light-industrial fringe zone, matching the farm/downtown boundary.
