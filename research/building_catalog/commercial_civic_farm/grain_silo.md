# Grain Silo (Farm-Scale)

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Farm zone, all settlement tiers — a standard farmstead feature, distinct from the town-scale `grain_elevator.md` (which is a commercial/rail-side structure). Farm silos are individual on-farm storage, usually sited right next to the dairy barn.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: Diameter 4-9m (farm-scale range; standard commercial farm diameters commonly 16', 18', 20', 24', 30' = ~4.9-9.1m).
- **Typical height**: 8-20m for farm-scale silos (small-scale 8-12m, medium up to 20m); very large examples exceed this but are atypical for a family farm.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Concrete stave construction (precast interlocking concrete blocks with steel hoop bands) is the classic tall cylindrical silo; newer steel/galvanized silos are also common. Domed or conical cap roof, external ladder running the full height, a vertical chute/door column for silage removal.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **No walkable interior rooms in the conventional sense.** The silo is a single tall cylindrical storage volume.
- Optional: small unloader room/access door at the base, 1.5m x 1.5m, where an auger or unloading mechanism sits.

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Base access point**: silage unloader/auger mechanism (1), small control panel, ladder base.
- **Interior volume**: treat as filled bulk silage/grain (set dressing only, not a furnished space) — optionally expose a cutaway or a partial-fill visual state for variety.

## Placement Notes
- Always sited immediately adjacent to the dairy barn (silage feeds livestock) — pair generation of `barn_dairy.md` instances with 1-3 silos nearby.
- External ladder and chute door column face the barn/feed-alley side for practical feed access.
- Not walkable/enterable in typical generation; treat as an exterior landmark/vertical-silhouette structure similar in generator role to `water_tower.md`, but farm-sited and smaller.
- Farms may have 1-4 silos in a row (a "silo bank") for a larger dairy operation — vary count by farm size tier.
