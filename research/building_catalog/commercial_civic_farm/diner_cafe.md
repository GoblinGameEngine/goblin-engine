# Diner / Cafe

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: All three tiers — a rural village has at most one small cafe; small towns and small cities may have 2-4.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 6-10m wide x 12-20m deep (20-35ft x 40-65ft) for a storefront cafe; a purpose-built prefabricated diner car-style building is smaller and freestanding, roughly 4m x 12m.
- **Typical story count**: 1 story.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Storefront cafe: brick block with large front window, often a projecting sign. Freestanding diner: stainless-steel/porcelain-enamel panel exterior, streamlined Moderne massing, small footprint set back from the street with parking.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Dining room**: 6-9m x 8-14m
- **Kitchen**: 4-6m x 5-8m, rear
- **Counter area (integrated into dining room or separate)**: 1.5m x 4-8m strip
- **Restroom(s)**: 1.5m x 2m each (1-2)
- **Small office/storage nook**: 2m x 2.5m, near kitchen
- **Walk-in cooler (small towns/cities)**: 2m x 2.5m, adjacent to kitchen

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Dining room**:
  - Booths (4-10)
  - Tables + chairs (2-4 top) (3-8 sets)
  - Counter with stools (6-12 stools, present in ~70% of instances)
  - Pie/dessert display case (1, near register)
  - Cash register/register stand (1)
  - Wall menu board or hanging chalkboard (1)
  - Coat hooks / wall art / taxidermy (rural variant) (a few)
- **Kitchen**:
  - Griddle/flat-top (1)
  - Range/oven (1)
  - Prep counters (2-3)
  - Deep fryer (0-1)
  - Coffee machine/urn (1, often visible from counter)
  - Sink stations (2)
  - Shelving for dry goods (2-3)
  - Kitchen pass-through window to counter/dining room (1)
- **Restroom**: toilet, sink.
- **Office/storage**: desk, shelving, mop sink.

## Placement Notes
- Counter, when present, always runs parallel to the kitchen pass-through so cooks can slide plates directly to counter seating.
- Kitchen is always rear-adjacent to the alley/service lane for deliveries and trash.
- Booths line the street-facing window wall for daylight and street visibility; tables fill the interior.
- Small-village cafes may omit the walk-in cooler and counter, using only tables/booths and a single reach-in fridge.
