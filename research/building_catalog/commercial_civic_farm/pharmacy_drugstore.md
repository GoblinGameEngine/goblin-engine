# Pharmacy / Drugstore

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Small towns and small cities primarily; rural villages may have the function folded into the general store instead of a dedicated pharmacy.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 8-13m wide x 15-22m deep (25-45ft x 50-70ft).
- **Typical story count**: 1 story, occasionally 2 (upper floor = doctor's office or apartment).
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Brick storefront, large display windows, classic pharmacy signage (mortar-and-pestle or neon script sign). Mid-century examples may have a soda-fountain counter visible from the street window.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Front sales floor (sundries/gifts)**: 6-9m x 8-12m
- **Pharmacy counter/dispensing area**: 3-5m x 4-6m, elevated or railed off at rear of sales floor
- **Soda fountain/lunch counter (optional, common pre-1970s)**: 3-4m x 5-8m
- **Stockroom**: 4-6m x 5-8m
- **Office/consultation nook**: 2-2.5m x 3m
- **Restroom**: 1.5m x 2m

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Front sales floor**: shelving (cosmetics, sundries, greeting cards) (5-9 units), gift/candy display cases (1-3), magazine rack (1), checkout counter (1).
- **Pharmacy counter**: dispensing counter with raised platform (1), pill-counting station, drawer cabinets (2-4), small safe for controlled substances (1), consultation window.
- **Soda fountain**: counter with stools (6-10 stools), syrup dispensers, malt mixer, backbar mirror/shelving, small tables (0-3, small city tier).
- **Stockroom**: shelving (pharmaceuticals, overstock) (4-6 units), refrigerator (1, for temperature-sensitive stock).
- **Office**: desk, filing cabinet.

## Placement Notes
- Pharmacy counter is always positioned at the rear of the store, farthest from the entrance, per real-world convention (forces customers past retail goods).
- Soda fountain, when generated, faces the street-front window for visibility and foot traffic draw; omit in rural-village tier instances built after ~1970 (generator flag).
- Restroom and stockroom sit at the rear, alley-adjacent.
