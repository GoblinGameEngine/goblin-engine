# Chicken Coop

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Farm zone, all settlement tiers — one of the smallest and most common farmstead outbuildings, present on nearly every farm regardless of size.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 3-6m x 4-8m for a family-farm-scale coop (larger commercial poultry houses exist but are atypical for the small-to-mid family farms this catalog targets — treat a larger poultry house as a rare small-city-adjacent-farm variant, up to 6m x 20m).
- **Typical story count**: 1 story, low eave height (~1.8-2.2m), sometimes with a partial loft for feed storage.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Wood-frame with board or shiplap siding, shed or low-gable roof, small door for human access plus a small low "pop-hole" door for the birds leading to an attached outdoor run, several small windows or screened vents for ventilation, often raised slightly off the ground on a low foundation or skids.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Main coop interior**: 3-6m x 4-8m, single room
- **Attached outdoor run (fenced, exterior)**: roughly matches or exceeds the coop footprint, 1.5-2x the interior area
- **Small feed storage nook/loft (optional)**: 1.5m x 2m

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Main coop interior**: nest boxes (wall-mounted row, 4-12 boxes), roosting bars/perches (2-4 bars), feed trough/hanging feeder (1-2), water fount (1-2), dropping board/pit beneath roosts (1), loose straw/bedding (floor covering, set dressing).
- **Feed storage nook**: feed sacks/bin (1-2), scoop.
- **Outdoor run**: wire fencing enclosure, small dust-bath depression (set dressing), occasional feeder/waterer duplicated outdoors.

## Placement Notes
- Pop-hole door always connects directly to the fenced outdoor run — keep this a short, direct path, not routed through the main human door.
- Nest boxes are placed along the wall away from the main door/high-traffic area for hen privacy (real husbandry convention worth preserving for authenticity, even though it's not gameplay-critical).
- Site close to the farmhouse (shortest egg-collection walk) but downwind/set apart from it, typically near the garden or a corner of the barnyard cluster.

## Sources
- General small-farm poultry-house construction convention (nest box/roost/pop-hole-to-run layout is standard across agricultural extension guidance); no single dimensioned historic-register source found, so figures reflect widely corroborated small-farm practice.
