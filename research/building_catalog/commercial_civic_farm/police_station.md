# Police Station (City Tier)

## Overview
- **Population tiers**: Small city (30,000-50,000) primarily — smaller settlements typically rely on a county sheriff substation or a single office folded into the town hall (see `town_hall.md`) rather than a dedicated police station. Small towns at the upper end (8,000-10,000) may have a minimal 2-3 room department; treat that as a scaled-down instance of this template rather than a separate archetype.
- **Typical exterior footprint**: 15-25m x 20-35m.
- **Typical story count**: 1-2 stories.
- **Typical exterior materials/architectural notes**: Brick or concrete civic/institutional building, fewer and smaller windows than typical civic buildings (security-conscious), often a secure sally-port vehicle entrance at the rear/side, municipal seal or "POLICE" signage prominent at the main entrance.

## Room/Space List (Template)
- **Public lobby/reception**: 4-6m x 6-8m
- **Dispatch/records counter**: 2m deep counter, 3-4m long, lobby-adjacent
- **Bullpen/detective desks**: 6-9m x 8-12m
- **Chief's office**: 3m x 3.5m
- **Interview/interrogation room**: 2.5m x 3m (1-2 rooms)
- **Holding cells**: 2m x 2.5m each, 2-6 cells, clustered with a small booking area
- **Booking area**: 3m x 4m, cell-adjacent
- **Evidence/property room**: 3m x 4m, secured
- **Locker room**: 4m x 5m
- **Break room**: 3m x 4m
- **Sally port/secure vehicle bay**: 4m x 8m, rear

## Per-Space Content Catalog
- **Lobby**: reception counter (1), bulletproof glass partition (0-1), waiting chairs (3-5), wanted-poster/notice board.
- **Dispatch**: radio console (1), multiple monitors, phone bank.
- **Bullpen**: desks (4-12), filing cabinets, shared printer/copier.
- **Interview room**: table, 2-3 chairs, recording equipment.
- **Holding cells**: bench/bunk (1 per cell), toilet (in-cell), cell doors (barred or solid steel).
- **Booking area**: fingerprint/booking desk (1), mugshot backdrop (1), property lockers.
- **Evidence room**: shelving (secured, 4-8 units), evidence lockers, log-in desk.
- **Locker room**: gym-style lockers (1 per officer, 10-40), benches.
- **Sally port**: patrol cars (2-6), secure roll-down door isolating it from the public lot.

## Placement Notes
- Holding cells and booking area are always windowless interior-core rooms, adjacent to the sally port for direct transfer from a patrol vehicle without crossing the public lobby.
- Public lobby and dispatch counter form the only zone accessible without escort; a secure door separates it from the bullpen/cells/evidence areas.
- Sally port is rear- or side-facing, away from the main street frontage.

## Sources
- General municipal police-station typology (secure zoning: public lobby / staff bullpen / secure booking-cell cluster) consistent with standard small-city civic building planning conventions.
