# Ranch House

## 1. Overview

- **Era**: c. 1945-1975 (peak 1950s-1960s); the dominant postwar suburban house type nationwide. Extremely common in Midwestern subdivisions built during postwar/baby-boom growth.
- **Population tier(s)**: Small towns (6,000-10,000) and small cities (30,000-50,000), typically in postwar subdivision-style residential zones with curving streets and larger lots than older grid neighborhoods.
- **Exterior footprint**: 12.2-18.3m x 9.1-12.2m (40-60 ft x 30-40 ft), often L-shaped or U-shaped with an attached garage extending the footprint further.
- **Story count**: 1 story (occasionally with a finished/unfinished basement below).
- **Exterior materials/roof**: Brick veneer, wood or aluminum siding; low-pitched gable or hip roof with wide overhanging eaves; attached 1-2 car garage; large picture window on the front facade; sliding glass door to a rear patio.

## 2. Room list (the template)

- Entry/foyer (small, often just a widened hallway point): 1.5-2.1m x 1.5-1.8m
- Living room: 3.7-5.5m x 3.4-4.6m (open to dining in many variants)
- Dining area/room (often an "L" off living room rather than fully separate): 2.7-3.7m x 2.7-3.4m
- Kitchen (often with an eat-in nook): 3.0-4.6m x 2.7-3.7m
- Family room (larger/later variants, informal second living space): 3.7-4.9m x 3.4-4.3m
- Primary bedroom: 3.4-4.3m x 3.4-3.7m
- Secondary bedroom(s) (x2-3): 3.0-3.4m x 2.7-3.0m
- Bathroom (full, hall/shared): 2.1-2.7m x 1.8-2.4m
- Bathroom (half, primary ensuite, later variants): 1.5-1.8m x 1.2-1.5m
- Attached garage (1-2 car, treat as semi-conditioned utility space): 3.0-6.1m x 5.5-6.1m
- Central hallway (bedroom wing spine): 0.9-1.2m wide

**Room count**: 6-9 total rooms; 3 bedrooms typical (2-4 range); 1-2 bathrooms. Overall footprint 100-145 sq m (1,080-1,565 sq ft) for the classic small ranch, larger for family-room variants.

## 3. Per-room content catalog

**Living room**: sofa (1), armchair(s) (1-2), TV/entertainment unit (1), coffee table (1), picture window seating/side tables (0-2), area rug (1), fireplace (0-1)

**Dining area**: dining table + chairs (4-6), sideboard/hutch (0-1), light fixture over table (1)

**Kitchen**: stove/range (1), refrigerator (1), sink + counter run (1), upper/lower cabinets, dishwasher (0-1, more common in later builds), eat-in breakfast nook table + chairs (0-1 set, 2-4 chairs), small appliances (1-3), trash can (1)

**Family room** (if present): sofa/sectional (1), TV/entertainment unit (1), armchair (0-2), games/hobby storage (0-1)

**Bedrooms (primary)**: bed - queen (1), nightstand(s) (2), dresser (1), closet (built-in sliding-door closet, standard) (1), desk (0-1)

**Bedrooms (secondary)**: bed - twin/full (1), nightstand (0-1), dresser (1), closet (built-in) (1), desk (0-1)

**Bathroom (full)**: toilet (1), sink/vanity (1), tub/shower combo (1), mirror (1), towel rack (1)

**Bathroom (half/ensuite)**: toilet (1), sink (1), mirror (1)

**Garage**: car(s) (1-2, implied/optional), workbench (0-1), storage shelving (1-2), lawn/garden tools, washer/dryer (0-1, sometimes located here or in a utility nook off garage)

## 4. Placement notes

- Strong "zoned" layout: public zone (living/dining/kitchen) on one side, private bedroom wing on the other, connected by a central hallway - a hallmark of the ranch plan.
- Kitchen typically adjacent to both the dining area and the garage/carport (for grocery unloading), often with a side or rear door.
- Attached garage is directly adjacent to the kitchen or a mudroom/utility entry, not the formal front door.
- Primary bedroom is usually at the opposite end of the bedroom wing from the secondary bedrooms for adult privacy, sometimes with the only ensuite half-bath.
- Living room commonly features a large picture window on the street-facing wall as the "showpiece" of the exterior.
- Sliding glass door at the rear (family room or dining area) opens onto a patio - place the family/dining zone against the rear exterior wall.
- L-shaped and U-shaped footprint variants wrap the garage or a wing around a front courtyard/driveway - useful as a footprint-shape variation knob for the generator.
